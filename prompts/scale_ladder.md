# The Scale Ladder — Scale-Spanning Worldbuilding Framework
*Owner ruling, 2026-08-23. The Library's altitude doctrine for prose volumes.*

A civilization isn't one thing at one size. The same questions get asked at every scale, but
the answers — and what even counts as a sensible answer — change completely. This framework
crosses the topic list against a scale ladder so every entry in the Library gets built at the
right altitude instead of everything defaulting to "kingdom-sized."

## The Scale Ladder

1. **Household / Family Unit** — the smallest coherent social atom
2. **Village / Settlement** — a few hundred to a few thousand people, one economy, one local government
3. **Region / Province** — multiple settlements under one authority, internal trade
4. **Nation / Kingdom / State** — full civilization, sovereign, has a foreign policy
5. **Continental / Cultural Sphere** — multiple nations sharing language family, religion, or trade bloc
6. **World** — the whole planet, all civilizations, the "setting" as usually understood
7. **Planar / Cosmological** — multiple worlds, planes of existence, the afterlife, elemental realms
8. **Meta** — rules that govern how magic/reality/story itself works across the setting
9. **Xeno** — civilizations that are NOT humanoid-baseline: alien logic, alien biology, alien values
10. **Omniverse** — the full multiverse; how different realities/timelines relate to each other at all

A topic doesn't have to be filled at every rung. But knowing which rung a question belongs to
prevents village-level detail bleeding into cosmology, or cosmic-scale abstractions leaving a
village feeling generic.

## How each topic reshapes across scale

### Governance & Power
| Scale | What the topic even means here |
|---|---|
| Household | Who has final say — elder, matriarch/patriarch, council of siblings |
| Village | Headman, council of elders, guild-master, single priest |
| Region | Governor/lord answering to a crown, regional council, warlord |
| Nation | Full political structure, succession law, courts |
| Cultural Sphere | Alliances, hegemons, shared legal traditions (e.g. a "commonwealth") |
| World | Balance of power between civilizations; dominant hegemon or true multipolarity |
| Planar | Who governs a plane — a god-king, a court of archons, no one at all |
| Meta | Are "rulers" even a coherent category, or does power work through some other mechanic entirely (narrative causality, belief-economy) |
| Xeno | Does this species even have "governance" as humans understand it — hive consensus, no concept of individual authority |
| Omniverse | Do different realities have any relationship at all — treaty, war, total unawareness |

### Economy & Resources
| Scale | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Household | Subsistence, barter with neighbors, inherited trade |
| Village | Local market, single dominant industry (mill, mine, port) |
| Region | Trade routes between settlements, taxation |
| Nation | Currency, banking, national industries, foreign trade |
| Cultural Sphere | Shared currency zones, trade blocs, common trade language |
| World | Global resource distribution, world-spanning trade networks |
| Planar | Resources that only exist cross-plane (soul-stuff, planar minerals) |
| Meta | Is scarcity even a rule of this setting, or is abundance/magic-negated economics the norm |
| Xeno | Alien value systems — a species that doesn't use "value" the way trade requires |
| Omniverse | Do resources/currency transfer between realities at all, or is each reality a closed economic system |

### Religion & Cosmology
| Scale | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Household | Ancestor shrine, personal patron spirit, private rites |
| Village | Local shrine/temple, folk practice, patron saint |
| Region | Regional cult, pilgrimage site |
| Nation | State religion, priesthood-crown relationship |
| Cultural Sphere | Shared pantheon/faith across multiple nations |
| World | Do all civilizations on the world agree the gods are real — and are they right |
| Planar | The gods' actual home plane; what divinity literally is, mechanically |
| Meta | Is "religion" downstream of literal cosmic truth here, or purely a belief-system with no confirmable referent |
| Xeno | A species whose relationship to the divine isn't worship at all — parasitism, symbiosis, indifference |
| Omniverse | Are the gods of one reality real/relevant in another; is there a god-of-gods above all realities |

### Military & Conflict
| Scale | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Household | Family feuds, personal honor disputes |
| Village | Militia, local disputes, banditry |
| Region | Regional levies, border skirmishes |
| Nation | Standing army, doctrine, full-scale war |
| Cultural Sphere | Wars between allied blocs, shared military traditions |
| World | World wars, apocalyptic-scale conflicts |
| Planar | Wars that span planes — invasions from the Abyss, planar crusades |
| Meta | Does conflict follow physical rules or narrative/thematic rules (prophecy-bound wars, story-logic duels) |
| Xeno | Conflict with something that doesn't wage "war" as understood — assimilation, infection, incomprehensible intent |
| Omniverse | Reality-vs-reality conflict; timeline wars; existential threats to the multiverse itself |

### Culture, Class & Identity
Same ladder logic — household customs → village folkways → national culture → shared
cultural-sphere identity → "is there a unifying world culture or total fragmentation" →
planar cultural differences (is a soul from the Feywild treated as a different "ethnicity" in
the mortal world) → meta (does culture even transmit the normal way, or through some
magical/narrative mechanism) → xeno (does this species have culture recognizable as such) →
omniverse (do parallel versions of "the same" culture diverge across realities, and does that
matter).

### Technology & Magic
| Scale | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Household | Personal tools, household charms |
| Village | Local craft-magic, blacksmith-enchanter |
| Region | Regional tech/magic specialization |
| Nation | State-level magical/technological infrastructure |
| Cultural Sphere | Shared schools of magic, tech standards |
| World | The setting's overall tech/magic ceiling |
| Planar | Magic that only functions on certain planes; plane-dependent physics |
| Meta | What are the actual rules of magic as a system — is it consistent, is it break-able, is it sentient |
| Xeno | A species whose "magic" isn't discrete from biology — magic as organ, not spell |
| Omniverse | Does magic work the same across realities, or is each branch running different physics |

### History & Memory
Household (family lore) → Village (founding story) → Region (old regional wars) → Nation
(founding myth, golden age, collapse) → Cultural Sphere (shared deep history, migrations) →
World (pre-history, lost ages) → Planar (history of the planes themselves, when they formed)
→ Meta (is history even linear here, or are timelines mutable/rewritten) → Xeno (does this
species even have a concept of "history" as sequential memory) → Omniverse (divergence points
— where did this reality split from another, and who remembers the fork).

### Language, Geography, Daily Life, Science
All follow the same pattern — scale them using the ladder as the template. Apply it to any
topic the same way: ask "what does this mean at a household? A world? A xeno species? An
omniverse?" and stop filling in rungs once the answer becomes "not applicable" or "not yet
relevant."

## The top three rungs, expanded

### 8. META — the rules governing the setting's reality itself
- Is magic a consistent physical law, a narrative force, or both simultaneously
- Does prophecy/fate exist as a binding mechanic, or just cultural belief
- Can the setting's "story logic" be exploited by characters who realize they're in one (genre-awareness as an in-world phenomenon)
- Is there a canon of "true" reality vs. false/illusory realities within the setting
- Do multiple magic systems coexist under one unifying meta-law, or are they truly separate systems that don't interact
- Is death permanent as a rule, or does the setting have soft-canon resurrection economics
- Who or what enforces the meta-rules — is there a Watcher, an author-figure, a cosmic balance-keeper

### 9. XENO — civilizations that break the humanoid-culture template
- Biology-first questions: does this species reproduce, age, or die the way cultures assume; does it have individuals at all or only a collective
- Sensory/cognitive divergence: does it perceive time linearly, does it have language as sound, does it have "self" as a bounded concept
- Value divergence: does it want anything recognizable as territory, wealth, offspring, legacy — or something with no human analogue
- Contact protocol: how do baseline civilizations even recognize this as a civilization rather than a natural phenomenon
- Translation problem: is genuine communication possible, or is everything mediated through lossy approximation
- Xeno-religion: does it worship, and if so is what it worships actually real by the setting's meta-rules

### 10. OMNIVERSE — reality-to-reality relationships
- Divergence architecture: do realities branch from shared points, or are they independent from creation
- Permeability: can beings/objects/information cross between realities, and at what cost
- Awareness: do civilizations within a reality know other realities exist, or is this purely a reader-level frame
- Hierarchy: is there a "prime" reality others are copies/branches of, or are all realities equally real
- Omniverse-scale threats: things that endanger not one world but the structure connecting all of them
- Omniverse-scale governance: is there any body, entity, or law operating above the level of individual realities

## How the Library uses this

**Cross-reference, don't duplicate.** A village's religion entry should link up to the Planar
entry for that god's actual home-plane, rather than re-explaining cosmology at every
settlement. Every topic is written at its own rung's altitude; a rung whose answer is "not
applicable" is left honestly empty rather than padded.
