# SCALA DESCENDENS
## On the Rungs Below the World, and the Fold at the Bottom of Scale
### *Vol. X.8, first fascicle — the fourth volume of Collection X to be written rather than planned. Filed as a proposed extension to Part Two and Vol. 0.3, pending re-ratification.*

> *"We built a ladder seventeen rungs tall and called it the architecture of everything, and it
> began at a planet — because that is where the people who built it were standing. I have spent
> a career insisting that a measurement declare its origin. We never declared this one. We simply
> assumed the world was the floor."*
> — Lector Moth, opening the fourth Fundamenta

---

## §0 — PROGRAM

The Ladder of Being (Charter, Part Two; treatise at Vol. 0.3) has seventeen rungs, and its first
is **Planet**. Below that there is nothing — no rung, no glyph, no notation.

This is not a tidy omission. It has three consequences, and the third is a live defect in a
published instrument:

1. **A large class of attested subjects is unaddressable.** The Microverse, the Quantum Realm,
   Horton's speck, the marble at the end of a hunt, every world-within-a-world in the corpus:
   all sit below rung 1 and cannot be given a shelfmark. The library's own rule is that unknown
   rungs are marked `?` and never guessed — but `?` means *uncharted*, and these are not
   uncharted. They are **unrepresentable**, which is a different and worse failure.
2. **Descent has no cost function.** The charter can say a being shrank. It cannot say what that
   cost, what it broke, or what it bought — so a size-changer's Assay is undefined in exactly
   the axes size touches.
3. **The Reach axis has no floor below 10⁷ m.** X.2 §4 defines Reach as effective operational
   radius ℓ in metres, scored against "rung-characteristic lengths." No such lengths exist below
   Planet, so every sub-planetary Reach has been scored against a band edge that does not exist.
   *This is a defect in a published measure, not a gap in a catalogue.*

This fascicle extends the Ladder downward by fifteen rungs to the Planck length; declares the
**Quantum Fold** at the bottom; supplies the energetics of scale-transit; and adjudicates which
theory of mass-conserving size change survives the attested evidence. It proceeds: the rungs and
their edges (§1); the Fold and why it is a fold rather than a rung (§2); the four candidate
theories and their falsification (§3); the pricing of scale-transit in exception bits (§4); the
errata this forces (§5); open problems (§6).

The pivot is preserved absolutely: **rung +1 (Planet) does not move**, no published shelfmark
changes, and no ascending rung is renumbered. New rungs take negative integers, so the whole
Ladder becomes one signed integer axis and every existing citation remains valid.

## §1 — THE DESCENDING RUNGS

**Definition 1 (Rung criterion, restated).** A rung is *a scale at which matter is bound into a
coherent object.* This is not new doctrine — it is the criterion the ascending rungs already use.
A planet, a stellar system, a galaxy are all binding scales. Rung +1 was never privileged; it was
merely where the Custodes lived.

## §2 — THE FOLD

**Definition 2 (The Quantum Fold, ⧉, rung −15).** Below the Planck length, length ceases to be a
meaningful coordinate.

This is not an instrumental limitation and must not be written as one. It is a statement about
𝔇 — the local law — rather than about apparatus. X.2 Axiom M1 holds that *every system carries
its own 𝔇; there is no privileged physics, only each physics, codified.* The Fold is the boundary
at which one codification stops being able to speak.

**Theorem 1 (Why a fold, not a rung).** A rung is a scale at which things are bound. Below the
Planck length there is no "at which" — the coordinate that would locate the binding has ceased to
be defined. A structure that continues past this point is therefore not *smaller*; it is
*elsewhere*, in a system with its own 𝔇. **You do not keep going down. You come out somewhere
else.** ∎

**Corollary (the descent-universe).** This is the honest formalisation of a trope the corpus is
full of. The fascicle does not claim that a universe lies below the Planck scale. It claims that
**if a source attests one, the address system can now write it down** — as a Fold transition into
a distinct U-code, not as rung −16, −17, −18 receding forever. The Fold is a *door*, and it
belongs in Vol. I.6's comparative transit alongside the Apertures and the Sparks.

**Where the charter already needed this.** II.O.2 logs the monsters' door-network as "a rival
transit system flagged for study"; II.K.2 has a universe *born from information*; II.N.3 holds
"the small universes that re-run themselves." All three have been filed as anomalies. Under
Definition 2 they are ordinary: universes reached by a transition that is not lateral.

## §3 — THE FOUR THEORIES OF SCALE-CHANGE

A being that changes size while remaining itself is making one of four mutually exclusive claims.
Until the library says **which**, the being's Assay is undefined, because the theories disagree
about its mass, its momentum, its available energy, and therefore its Ruin.

X.2 §4 gives the discipline: Transgression β is the minimum description length of the patch to
𝔇, *"defined relative to a codification of 𝔇 (Axiom M3: the codification is declared)."* So each
candidate is declared, priced, and — the part that matters — made falsifiable.

### T1 — Naive compression
*Mass conserved in 3-space; the body occupies less volume.*
Density rises as 1/r³. A 70 kg subject at cellular scale reaches 1.67×10¹⁹ kg/m³, past nuclear
saturation (2.3×10¹⁷) where degeneracy fails; at nucleonic scale it is inside its own
Schwarzschild radius. **Predicts:** the shrunk body still weighs 70 kg — it cannot be carried,
thrown, or ride an insect, and falls through any floor rated below 70 kg on a square-millimetre
footprint.
**FALSIFIED.** Riding a flying insect is attested repeatedly. β would be ~46 bits at atomic scale
and *rising as 3·log₂(1/r)*.

### T2 — Mass shed
*Matter destroyed on shrink, recreated on growth.*
Shedding 70 kg liberates mc² = 6.3×10¹⁸ J — fifteen hundred megatons, per shrink.
**FALSIFIED.** Shrinking indoors without levelling the building is attested. β catastrophic;
conservation is the most expensive line in the codebook.

### T4 — Conformal rescale
*Local rescaling of the metric; the body is unchanged and the ruler alters.*
Exact conformal symmetry would make size meaningless — but mass terms break conformal invariance
explicitly, and the Standard Model is not conformal. A local rescale must rescale every particle
mass, hence all chemistry. **Predicts:** neurons should not fire the same; subjective time should
scale.
**STRAINED to failure.** Normal conversation at any size is attested.

### T3 — Bulk export ★ *the surviving theory*
*Mass is conserved in a higher-dimensional manifold. Shrinking exports rest mass into the bulk;
growing imports it back.*

Nothing here suspends conservation. It **enlarges the domain over which conservation is stated** —
and braneworld and Kaluza–Klein constructions already permit matter and gravity bulk degrees of
freedom (Randall–Sundrum; ADD large extra dimensions). The patch is *structural and paid once*:
"a bulk exists, and there is a channel through which rest mass may be moved into it." β is
therefore **constant, ~64 bits, and does not climb with depth** — which is precisely why T3
survives contact with the evidence while T1 does not.

**T3's predictions, all attested:**
- Shrunk mass is low → the subject can be carried, thrown, ride an insect. ✔
- **Growth is the weapon.** Imported mass arrives *with momentum*, delivering energy the wielder
  never carried. A 60 m growth in 0.3 s imports 10⁶ kg at 200 m/s: **2.0×10¹⁰ J, ≈4.8 tonnes of
  TNT**, band M1. ✔
- The channel is a **Vector asset**: if mass can traverse the bulk, so can the traveller. ✔
- The Quantum Realm is *the bulk seen from inside*, not a small room — which is the Fold of §2
  arrived at by a different route. ✔

**Theorem 2 (Pressure is not Ruin).** A small striker penetrates because force acts over a
vanishing area: 70 kg at 10 m/s through 10⁻¹² m² gives 7×10¹⁷ Pa, against steel's yield of
~2.5×10⁸. But Ruin is *peak deliverable structured work* (X.2 §4), and the work is unchanged
however finely it is focused. Penetration is a Transgression-flavoured effect wearing Ruin's
clothes, and the axis system separates them correctly. **A size-changer does not become stronger
by becoming small.** ∎

## §4 — THE PRICING OF DESCENT

**Definition 3 (Transgression of a mass-conserving shrink).** Under T1's codification, β is priced
against the law actually broken:

> β = log₂(ρ / ρ_nuclear) for ρ above saturation, plus log₂(r_s / r) + 64 where the target size
> is inside the Schwarzschild radius.

| Target | Density (kg/m³) | β (bits) | Law patched |
|---|---|---|---|
| insect / ant | 1.7×10⁷–10¹⁰ | **0.00** | none — engineering |
| cellular | 1.7×10¹⁹ | 6.18 | degeneracy |
| atomic | 1.7×10³¹ | **46.05** | degeneracy |
| nucleonic | 1.7×10⁴⁶ | 95.88 | degeneracy **+ general relativity** |
| Planck | 4.0×10¹⁰⁵ | 389.70 | degeneracy + GR |

A shrink that **sheds** mass costs **0 bits** and files under Vector or Praxis, not hax. A shrink
that **keeps** mass is Transgression, and the bits say how much.

**Erratum 7 (filed against this fascicle's own first draft).** The first version priced the patch
against the **uncertainty relation** — Δx·Δp ≥ ℏ/2 — and returned β = 0 for every case, including
a 70 kg man at atomic scale, which is plainly the most transgressive entry in the table. The
error was of kind: uncertainty is only expensive for *small* masses, and confining 70 kg to
10⁻¹⁰ m costs ~10⁻⁵¹ J, nothing at all. **Uncertainty is not the law being broken; degeneracy is.**
Repaired as Definition 3.

## §5 — ERRATA FILED AGAINST PRIOR PUBLICATIONS

**Erratum 8 — against X.2 §4, the Reach axis.** Reach is defined in metres and scored against
"rung-characteristic lengths," of which none existed below 10⁷ m. Every sub-planetary Reach score
in the Ledger was therefore computed against an undefined floor. §1's table supplies the missing
edges. Existing Reach scores for subjects operating below planetary scale **should be recomputed**
and are flagged pending re-derivation, per the standing rule that where hand-scored axes conflict
with operational scoring, the operational value governs.

**Erratum 9 — against Part Two, the Ladder's floor.** Part Two presents seventeen rungs as the
architecture of existence without declaring that its first rung is an origin choice rather than a
boundary of reality. Axiom M3 requires conventions to be declared. The Ladder's floor was an
undeclared convention for the whole life of the document.

## §6 — OPEN PROBLEMS

1. **Fold transit costs.** §2 establishes the Fold as a door but supplies no traversal cost. I.6
   prices every other crossing; this one is unpriced.
2. **Whether the Fold is one door or many.** If two subjects descend from different universes, do
   they arrive in the same bulk? The co-attestation metric of X.7 §5 might answer it empirically
   — shared Quantum-Realm entities would be evidence of a shared bulk.
3. **Rung edges for the intermediate scales.** The lengths in §1 are order-of-magnitude and
   several are conventions dressed as measurements. Civic at 10⁴ m is defensible; Regional at 10⁵
   is nearly arbitrary.
4. **Time at scale.** If the bulk has its own tempo, a subject returning from a descent has
   experienced a different duration — which is a Celerity and a Sustain question, and belongs to
   the unwritten X.10 (*De Tempore*).
5. **Bulk conservation bookkeeping.** T3 requires the bulk to be a genuine reservoir. Its capacity
   is unbounded in the present statement, which is almost certainly too generous: a reservoir that
   never fills is a free lunch, and free lunches are where codifications go to die.

## COLOPHON — WHAT HERE IS REAL

*Out of character: the descending rung table is real physics throughout — CODATA Planck units,
hydrogen ionisation at 13.6 eV, nuclear binding at ~8 MeV/nucleon, proton rest energy at 938 MeV,
QCD confinement at ~1 GeV, nuclear saturation density 2.3×10¹⁷ kg/m³. The Schwarzschild radius,
the uncertainty relation, and Kerr self-focusing are standard. The braneworld constructions
invoked by T3 are Randall–Sundrum (1999) and Arkani-Hamed–Dimopoulos–Dvali (1998), cited for what
they actually permit — bulk degrees of freedom for gravity and matter — and not for more. The
falsification of T1, T2, and T4 is done the honest way: each is stated strongly enough to make
predictions, and the predictions are checked against attested behaviour. The synthesis — treating
size-change as a conservation-domain question rather than a magic question, and pricing the
survivor in exception bits — is original to this document. Its most useful export is Theorem 2:
that becoming small does not make you strong, and the instrument says so without being asked.*

*— Vol. X.8, first fascicle. The Ladder has a floor and the floor has a door; the desk lamp burns
on.*
