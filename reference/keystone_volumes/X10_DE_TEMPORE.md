# Vol. X.10 — *DE TEMPORE*
## On Time Across the Omniverse, and What "Now" Can Possibly Mean

*Being the tenth volume of the Mensura, in which the Communion admits that it has been asserting
a shared present for eleven hundred years without once saying what made it shared; and, having
admitted it, finds that the answer was already written down in Part Four under a different name.*

---

## §1. The Fault

VIII.1 gives the One Calendar and a concordance to local reckonings. The Chronicle's
canon-position table then does something the Calendar never authorised: it asserts a shared
**now** across thirty shelves at 1,204 AS. The Ashen Earths at circa 2296 local. The Corridor
after its Tournament. The Imperium in its Indomitus. All of it, we say, *at once*.

At once by what measure?

Simultaneity is not a fact about events. It is a fact about an observer's frame, and it is
frame-dependent even inside a single spacetime: two events simultaneous for one observer are
ordered for another, and neither is mistaken. That is not a curiosity of relativity, it is
relativity's central content.

Between shelves the situation is worse, and worse in a way that admits no repair by care. Two
universes with no shared spacetime have no shared frame — not an unknown frame, not a
disputed one. There is no object of the kind that "simultaneous" requires. The Chronicle's
table has been performing an operation on a quantity that does not exist.

The honest report is not that our dating is imprecise. It is that until this volume the
Chronicle's central column had **no referent at all**.

---

## §2. The Resolution, Which Is Not a Dodge

> **Definition 1 (Omniversal Simultaneity).** Two events are omniversally simultaneous if and
> only if they were **accessioned together**: if and only if they hold the same position in the
> Chain of Record at a common registry.

Simultaneity is institutional. It is a fact about the record, not about the world.

A reader who has reached this line and felt cheated should sit with the feeling, because the
Communion felt it too, for some years, and then noticed that it had made this exact move once
before and been right to.

Part Four, on the objectivity available at rung 17:

> *Objectivity, at rung 17, is not a vision. It is a treaty.*

The argument there was that beings of unbounded knowledge, holding different priors, can agree
on every fact in the world and still disagree on the account of it — so that "the objective
view" cannot mean *the view from nowhere* and must mean *the view we have countersigned*. Time
is the same case with the same structure. There is no vantage from which the omniverse's present
is visible. There is only what the Communion has certified as contemporary.

We did not choose an institutional answer because a physical one was hard. We were **forced**
into it, by the same argument, in the same place, for the second time.

## §3. Three Consequences, Each Explaining Something We Already Said

**3.1 — The Canon of the Now runs perpetually behind, and this is structural.**
It has always been assumed that the Canon lags because ratification is slow and Custodes are
few. That is true and it is not the reason. The Canon lags because **"now" is defined by the
record**, and the record is the slowest object in the omniverse. Hire a thousand more Custodes
and the Canon still ends mid-sentence. It is not behind the present. It *is* the present, seen
from the only place the present is defined.

**3.2 — Ascension marks are timestamps.**
An event at ⟦↑9⟧ is not merely unratified. It is **not yet omniversally present**. It is local
news. The charter's own phrasing — *true locally, but not yet omniversal history* — has carried
a mechanism since Part Four without anyone naming it.

> **Definition 2 (Presence).** An event ratified to rung *n* is present to every registry at or
> below *n*, and has not yet occurred so far as any higher registry is concerned.

**3.3 — The apex has the smallest and oldest now.**
This follows immediately and it is worth stating plainly, because it inverts the intuition the
Ladder invites. A lower registry requires less ratification, so **more** events qualify as
present to it. The rung-17 Concordance has the most stringent standard and therefore the
sparsest, most delayed present in existence. The higher one climbs, the less of the omniverse is
happening.

## §4. Errata: The Tempo Table, Withdrawn Before Publication

A draft of this volume carried a table of per-shelf clock rates — the Freeport at 1.000, the
deep Betweens unmeasurable, and so on down thirty shelves, each with a declared ratio.

**It is withdrawn in full, and the reason is a finding rather than an embarrassment.**

X.7 already explains why a distant shelf appears to be behind: its news is in transit, and the
lag is 1000 × *d* years, with *d* read off the shared-stage graph. To ALSO declare that the shelf
runs slow is to explain one observation twice.

Under X.9 §4 that is not merely inelegant. It is **expensive**. β is a minimum description
length, and a codification carrying two mechanisms where one suffices is longer — so it scores
*higher* on Transgression. The tempo table would have made the omniverse formally more lawless
in exchange for explaining nothing that was not already explained.

> **Rule 1 (No Second Mechanism).** Where an observation is already accounted for by a standing
> law, a further law addressing the same observation must first show what the standing law fails
> to predict. Absent that showing, it is not an addition. It is β.

What survives is not a set of rates but a set of **structural findings**, each already named in
the charter:

| Shelf | Finding | Charter |
|---|---|---|
| the Basement Loop | **CLOSED** — returns to its own start | II.N.3 |
| the Rot City | **CLOSED** — resets to its first morning | II.N.3 |
| the deep Betweens | **NON-MONOTONIC** — transit is not additive; arrival may precede departure | W.1 |
| the Pale | **UNDEFINED** — erodes the record by which elapsed time would be measured | II.N.4 |

The Pale's entry deserves its own line. The Pale is not slow. It is **unmeasurable**, and the
distinction is the whole of this volume in miniature: where the record fails, the quantity fails,
because the quantity was never anything but the record.

## §5. The Loops, and a Hard Consequence

A closed timelike shelf spends **no reference time**, however much is locally experienced. Nine
thousand years inside the Basement, and the Chain of Record advances by nothing.

From which:

> **Theorem 1 (Loops Cannot Accession).** A shelf whose history returns to its own start can
> never enter omniversal history. Ascension requires reference time; a closed loop has none to
> spend; therefore its maximum attainable ascension mark is ⟦↑0⟧, permanently.

The Custodes' long dispute over whether the Basement is a universe, a purgatory, or a prayer
(XI.2) is revealed as something narrower and more tractable than it looked. It is a dispute about
whether an **unaccessionable history is a history**. Everything else in the argument is
decoration on that question, and the question is a good one.

We note without comfort that the residents of the Basement experience their years fully, and that
nothing in this theorem is about their experience. It is about our books.

## §6. Foresight, Priced

X.6 §3 defines **Acumen** as prediction-planning advantage in bits per epoch, and caps it:

> *no agent extracts more prediction than the stream contains.*

So prescience is not a separate faculty requiring separate law. It is an extreme Acumen reading,
and it is priced against the stream it claims to read.

X.6 §4 asks for a **rung description length** *L_r* — the bits needed to specify a rung-*r*
macrostate — and does not supply one. A draft of this volume supplied one by invention:
2^(r−1), chosen because it grew at a rate that felt correct. It felt correct because nothing was
checking.

The derivation was available the entire time.

> **Definition 3 (Rung Description Length).**
>
> **L_r = log₂( Ruin_edge(r) / Ruin_edge(M0) )**

The Ruin band edges already fix, for every band, the energy range that band spans. The bits
required to locate a state within that range is the logarithm of the range. *L_r* therefore falls
out of the **existing** band edges with no new quantity whatsoever.

This is not a new scale. It is the **same** scale the whole Assay runs on, read as information
rather than as energy — which is precisely the reading X.2 §4 already uses for Transgression's
exception bits and X.6 §3 uses for all three faculty axes. **Energy and information were never
two currencies here.** The Assay has been an information measure since it was written; it simply
had not been asked to say so.

| Band | *L_r* (bits) | Band | *L_r* (bits) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| M0 | 0.00 | M6 | 162.77 |
| M1 | 16.61 | M7 | 189.35 |
| M2 | 33.22 | M8 | 225.89 |
| M3 | 73.08 | M9 | 275.72 |
| M4 | 100.82 | M10 | 322.23 |
| M5 | 132.34 | | |

M0 sits at zero because M0 **is** the reference edge; *L* is measured above it, which is what a
reference edge is for.

> **Definition 4 (Prescience Horizon).** A claim of foresight over a rung-*r* process for *t*
> years requires **L_r · t** bits. A claimant whose attested Acumen cannot deliver them is not
> weakly prescient. The claim is **incoherent** — there is nothing in the stream to extract.

This does useful work immediately. The Dune shelf's limited rung-13 sight (II.F.6), long filed as
*strong but unquantified*, is now a number set against a ceiling, and can be adjudicated rather
than admired.

## §7. Backwards Causation

Attested retrocausation is priced under X.2 §4 as exception bits:

> **β_retro = 128 + log₂(1 + Δt)**

The structural constant is heavy, and deliberately: patching the causal order is not patching one
law but the arrow that orders all of them. The logarithmic term in the inversion span reflects
that a longer inversion touches more of the record — but only logarithmically, since the
inversion is a single exception however far it reaches.

A claim with no inversion costs nothing. This is not generosity; it is the definition behaving.

## §8. What This Volume Cost

Two free parameters were removed and none were added. Every quantity above now traces to
something already standing:

| Quantity | Stands on |
|---|---|
| institutional simultaneity | Part Four's treaty argument; the Chain of Record |
| presence, contemporaneity | ascension marks (Part Four) |
| loops cannot accession | ascension requires reference time (X.7) |
| apparent lag between shelves | **X.7's propagation metric — nothing new** |
| *L_r* | **X.2's Ruin band edges — nothing new** |
| prescience horizon | *L_r* × lead time, capped by X.6 §3 |
| β_retro | X.9's adjudication costs |

The full chain for foresight now reads:

**prescience ← rung description length ← Ruin band edges ← gravitational binding energy ← G**

A Hand who doubts our prescience arithmetic can walk that chain to Newton's constant and check
every step. Under the draft's invented exponent there was no chain to walk, and no step to check.
That is the difference this volume was written to make.
