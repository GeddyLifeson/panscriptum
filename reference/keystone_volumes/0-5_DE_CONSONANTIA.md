## §0 — PROGRAM

Two questions this volume settles, both of which the library has been operating on without stating.

**First: what the Order's method actually is.** The Vade Mecum teaches the *grammar* — how to read,
write, and amend. Collection X supplies the *instruments*. Neither says what the Custodes believe
about knowledge such that the grammar takes this shape. Why does a Contradictions register exist at
every rung? Why is a divergent reading filed with both signatures rather than averaged? Why is
"unresolved," written in a steady hand, the mark of the fourth literacy rather than of failure?

**Second: why Custodes disagree.** They are not divided into schools. **Resonantism is the school
of all Custodes**, and by its own non-sectarian structure it cannot fracture into rivals: every
branch stays in resonance with the root, and disagreement deepens identity rather than destroying
it. So a doctrinal explanation of Custodial dispute is unavailable — and yet Avar and Quill read
the same man and file different numbers.

This volume's thesis: **they disagree at the granularity of bias, not at the level of doctrine** —
and the disagreement is formally characterisable, permanent, and *load-bearing*. It is not noise
around a signal. It is the reason the ratified record has to be a treaty.

## §1 — THE ONTOLOGY, AND WHY IT WAS ALREADY HERE

*A first draft of this volume treated Resonantism as an epistemology — an account of how Custodes
know, cashed out in priors. That is true, and it is secondary. Filed as Erratum 11 against this
volume's own opening.*

Resonantism's first line is not about knowing. **Being is constituted by relation.** That is a
claim about what things *are*. Read that way, it is not an import into the charter's mathematics.
**It is what that mathematics found and could not name.**

### Four places the library is already relational

**Power is never a property.** X.2 Definition 4 defines the capability preorder:

> **A ⪰ B iff C_B ⊆ C_A** — A can do everything B can, at least as fast, at least as reliably.

Strength is a *relation between agents*. Nothing anywhere in the charter defines an agent's power
intrinsically, because there is no such quantity to define. A Moth Number is a projection of a
relation, never a possession. *The instrument was relational before anyone said the word.*

**Incomparability is a finding, not missing data.** Proposition 1 establishes agents with
**A ⋡ B and B ⋡ A** — two things, both true, neither greater. This is **Coexistent Contradiction
stated as an order-theoretic fact**, and Theorem 1 (no lossless scalarization) is its consequence:
a total order cannot represent a partial one, so every scalar is a projection and projection
destroys information *necessarily, not accidentally*.

Measured on the charter's own worked cases, **67% of pairs are incomparable** — including
Goku ⋡ Jace and Jace ⋡ Goku, which is Part Four's model example arriving as arithmetic.

**The curl is the residue of relation.** X.2 §5 decomposes contest flow as
**F = grad(θ) ⊕ curl ⊕ harmonic**. The gradient is the best-fitting ladder. The curl is
irreducible non-transitivity — the cycles where A beats B beats C beats A, which no ordering
survives.

**And Moth said it outright.** The consistency index **η = ‖grad F‖² / ‖F‖²** measures the
fraction of relational reality a ladder can represent at all, and X.2 §5 states in plain text:

> *"the omniverse is not a ladder; it is a chord" is the statement η < 1, and η is measurable.*

**Theorem 0 (η is the coexistent-contradiction index).** A chord is simultaneous distinct tones
held in tension without resolving into one. η is the degree to which relation *does* resolve into
ranking; **1 − η is the degree to which it does not, and cannot.** Theorem 2 bounds every scalar
assay's error below by that residue. So Coexistent Contradiction is not a doctrine the library
adopts — it is a **measurable quantity the library's own instrument reports**, and the instrument
has been reporting it since the first Fundamenta. ∎

| Contest structure | η | Ladder represents | Irreducibly chord |
|---|---|---|---|
| pure transitive order | 1.000 | 100% | 0% |
| one upset in a ladder | 0.499 | 49.9% | 50.1% |
| rock–paper–scissors | **0.000** | **0%** | **100%** |

**Corollary (what the Order has been doing).** A librarian who files both signed readings, refuses
to average them, and writes "unresolved" in a steady hand is not being humble. They are declining
to project a partial order onto a line, **because they have an instrument that tells them how much
would be destroyed if they did.**

### The connective language

Once Being is relation, every system in the library is the same claim at a different scale, and
the sameness is not analogy — it is identity of form:

| System | The relation it measures |
|---|---|
| **The Chord** (I.7, X.9) | one substrate; entities are instruments, and power is *drawing on a shared account* |
| **The capability preorder** (X.2) | strength as relation between agents |
| **η and the curl** (X.2 §5) | how much relation exceeds ranking |
| **The Identity Rule** (Part Four) | a thing is what it is *witnessed as*, across witnesses — identity itself is relational |
| **The shared-stage graph** (X.7) | how strongly two shelves are in relation, as a number |
| **Propagation** (X.7 §5) | news travels along resonance; distance *is* weakness of relation |
| **The Ladder** (Part Two, X.8) | every rung is a *binding* — a scale at which relation holds matter together |
| **Attestation** (Vade Mecum II.4) | the relation between a claim and its witness |

**Theorem 0b (the library is one object).** These are not nine systems sharing a metaphor. They
are one ontological commitment — *relation is prior to relata* — expressed at nine scales. The
weave does not *resemble* the Chord; the weave is the Chord, counted. Propagation is not *like*
resonance; propagation is resonance, timed. ∎

*The epistemology that follows in §2 is downstream of this. Because relation is prior, a knower is
also constituted relationally — has a standpoint, a prior, a partition — and that is why Custodes
who share one method still diverge. The Bayesian account is true because the ontology is.*

## §2 — THE METHOD

Resonantism holds that **Being is constituted by relation.** Applied to a library, four axioms
follow, and each is already visible in the library's construction.

**Relational Meditation.** Identity is co-created through relation; there is no pre-relational
essence waiting to be catalogued. *In the library:* an entry is not a description of a thing in
isolation but of a thing in its web — which is why the Aperture Doctrine (I.9) makes an entry's
scope a function of its subject's reach, and why a Thread is a citation rather than an ornament.

**Coexistent Contradiction.** Mutually incompatible positions can and often must coexist. *In the
library:* the Contradictions register, at every rung, up to and including ⟦↑17⟧. The Compact of
Mirrors — *"both are true at their addresses"* — is this axiom as case law.

**Engaging Resonance.** Value arises from engagement; indifference produces a self alive
biologically and absent ontologically. *In the library:* Quill goes there and bleeds on the page;
Avar corrects in the margin. The marginalia Hands are not decoration. They are the axiom's
staffing.

**Cyclical Impermanence.** Collapse is not failure but the precondition of rebuilding. *In the
library:* the errata regime; the Five Ages; the Athenaeum Ultima itself — a cathedral of ink that
became a ruin and sent scaffolding through a closing door.

**Theorem 1 (The method is already installed).** Every structural peculiarity of the Panscriptum's
epistemology — contradictions filed rather than resolved, both signatures retained, `?` never
guessed, "unresolved" as a mark of mastery — is a consequence of these four axioms. The Order has
been practising Resonantism since its founding and describing it as procedure. ∎

## §3 — WHY, THEN, DO CUSTODES DISAGREE?

If the method is universal and cannot fracture, dispute needs another source. It has one, and it
is not a defect of the Order but a theorem about inference.

X.2 §7 already declares the Ledger **Bayesian throughout**: a band-population prior, a likelihood
from feats and contests, a posterior on the axis vector. That machinery has a known and permanent
property, and the Order has been living inside it without naming it.

**Definition 1 (The three granularities).** Two Custodes running the same method may still differ
in:

- **π — the prior.** What is held plausible *before* the evidence. A Custos raised among the
  Gyre's ascending champions and one raised among the Grim Dark's attritional war do not begin
  from the same expectations about what a person can become.
- **ℒ — the admissibility.** What counts as evidence at all, and how strongly. Attestation grades
  formalise part of this — Witnessed narrow, Reconstructed wide — but the residue is real: one
  Custos reads a native scripture as testimony, another as literature.
- **𝒬 — the partition.** How finely the world is cut into events before anything is counted. Two
  Custodes who disagree about whether a battle was one event or three will disagree about its
  frequency, its causes, and its weight, having observed the very same battle.

**Theorem 2 (Same evidence, different posterior).** For any body of evidence E, agents with priors
π₁ ≠ π₂ compute posteriors P₁(·|E) ≠ P₂(·|E). This is not error. It is Bayes' rule operating
correctly on different inputs. ∎

**Theorem 3 (The convergence limit — why this never goes away).** The standard consolation is that
evidence washes out priors: by the martingale convergence results (Doob) and the merging theorem
of Blackwell and Dubins, two Bayesians *do* converge as evidence accumulates — **provided their
priors are mutually absolutely continuous.** That proviso is the whole story. It requires that the
two agents agree about what is *possible*: that neither assigns probability zero to something the
other allows.

> **Where a Custos assigns zero, no quantity of evidence will ever move them, and no quantity of
> evidence *should* — conditioning on a measure-zero set is undefined.** Two Custodes who differ
> on what is possible will diverge forever while both reason impeccably. ∎

**Corollary (the granularity of bias).** Custodial dispute is therefore not a disagreement about
facts and not a schism about method. It is a difference in π, ℒ, and 𝒬 — *the granularity at which
each has cut the world before beginning* — and it is permanent wherever the priors are not mutually
absolutely continuous. **Different schools would be a solvable problem. This is not.**

## §4 — WHAT THIS EXPLAINS THAT NOTHING ELSE DID

**The Grand Custodes.** Part Four states that omniscient beings keep records because *"omniscience
does not abolish perspective. To know everything is still to know it as someone — total knowledge
is still indexed from a standpoint, weighted by a temperament, ordered by a history of
attention."* Two omniscient beings *"can agree on every fact in existence and still disagree on
cause, weight, and meaning."*

Under §3 this stops being a paradox and becomes a theorem. Omniscience saturates **ℒ** — the
evidence is complete, and there is nothing further to admit. It does nothing whatsoever to **π** or
**𝒬**. A being that knows every fact still had to arrive with a prior and still had to cut the
world into events before counting them. *Total evidence does not confer a shared prior, and a
shared prior is what convergence requires.*

**Objectivity as treaty.** Hence Part Four's conclusion — *"Objectivity, at rung 17, is not a
vision. It is a treaty"* — is exactly right and now has its proof. A ratified record is not what
any observer sees; it is what all of them have countersigned, because seeing is prior-relative all
the way up and countersigning is the only thing that is not.

**Why ⟦↑17⟧ events keep Contradictions registers.** Contradiction is native to the top of the
chain, not residue from the bottom. Ratification height measures *agreement to file*, never
convergence of belief.

**The Custodial Paradox, restated exactly.** The Custos Absolute's eternal work — *choosing what
the record shall say without that choice being bias, when refusing to choose is also a choice* —
is now nameable: **it is the problem of selecting a prior.** There is no uninformative prior; the
attempt to be neutral is itself a choice of parametrisation, and a different parametrisation gives
a different "neutral." The desk with one item on it holds the reference-prior problem, and the
Panscriptum's response — *"the Panscriptum does not solve it. The Panscriptum cites it"* — is the
correct response to a problem with no interior solution.

## §5 — THEOREM 4: THE HANDS ARE THE PRIORS, AND THE ± IS THEIR SPREAD

The four recurring marginalia Hands have been read for a century as the library's charm — the
dry institution, the enthusiast who went there, the one who models everything, the one who only
warns. They are not charm. **They are the Order's canonical priors**, and this is the volume's
central finding.

| Hand | Prior |
|---|---|
| **CUSTOS-PRIME AVAR** | *institutional* — the accumulated base rates; weights the ratified record heavily; corrective by disposition |
| **SISTER QUILL** | *experiential* — went there, saw it, bled on the page; weights witnessed testimony heavily and distrusts the ledger's smoothing |
| **LECTOR MOTH** | *formal* — the worksheet, the fitted weights, the operational scoring rule; distrusts anything not recomputable |
| **THE UNNAMED HAND** | *tail-risk* — weights the catastrophic tail far above the others, and speaks only where the tail is what matters |

**Theorem 4 (The interval is prior divergence, made numeric).** The published ± of an Assay is
not measurement error and not, principally, thin evidence. It is *the spread of the Hands'
readings* — the distance between the Order's own priors, converted into a number and printed.

*The library has been saying this and not hearing itself.* X.2 §7 sources the interval from
"attestation grade **and source disagreement**." Part Three states that "the confidence bounds are
where the library stores its power-scaling disputes — Avar's and Quill's competing readings of Son
Goku are both filed, both signed." Absolute 3 forbids silent averaging. And the Vade Mecum's
countersign check (III.4) requires *"that the interval covers both signed readings"* — a
constraint that makes no sense if ± is noise, and perfect sense if ± is the envelope of the
signatures. ∎

**Reconstruction, against the charter's own published ledger.** Taking plausible per-Hand readings
and deriving the interval as the signature envelope, added in quadrature to the attestation floor:

| Subject | Hand readings | Derived | Charter prints | Prior share of variance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Son Goku, MUI | AVAR 7.41 · QUILL 7.98 · MOTH 7.55 | 𝔄 M7.65 ± 0.34 | M7.62 ± 0.41 | **89%** |
| The Emperor, Throne-bound | AVAR 5.62 · QUILL 6.88 · MOTH 5.95 | 𝔄 **M6.15** ± 0.75 | **M6.15** ± 0.85 | 71% |
| Kenshiro, post-Raoh | AVAR 3.52 · QUILL 3.58 · MOTH 3.49 | 𝔄 M3.53 ± 0.11 | M3.52 ± 0.12 | 17% |

**Corollary 1 (why Goku is argued and Kenshiro is not).** Goku's dispute is 89% prior divergence:
Quill saw the Tournament of Power and weights it; Avar weights the ratified chain; Moth weights
the worksheet. No further feat closes that gap, because the gap is not about feats. Kenshiro's
interval is 17% prior and 83% attestation floor — **his ± would genuinely narrow with more
fieldwork, and Goku's would not.** Two intervals of similar appearance, two entirely different
objects, and until now the notation could not tell them apart.

**Corollary 2 (the Emperor, explained twice over).** X.2 §5 attributes his ±0.85 to a sparse
contest graph. §3 of this volume attributes Custodial spread to divergent priors. Both are true
and they compound: an entity read across a graph too sparse to constrain anyone is precisely where
priors do the most work, because there is least evidence to overrule them. *He is the most argued
name in the Registry because he is the name about which the Order's own priors have the least to
push against.*

**Corollary 3 (a new required field).** Every published interval should carry its **prior share**.
An interval that is 17% prior is a purchase order for fieldwork. An interval that is 89% prior is
a standing statement about the Order, and no fieldwork will fill it. Printing them identically has
been costing the Order effort aimed at results that cannot arrive.

## §6 — THE DISCIPLINE THAT FOLLOWS

If dispute is structural, the Order's obligations are not to eliminate it but to make it legible.

1. **Declare the prior.** A worksheet states the band-population prior it began from. Axiom M3
   already demands conventions be declared before use; this extends it to the one convention most
   often left silent.
2. **Declare admissibility.** Attestation grades carry part of this; where a Custos admits a source
   another would reject, the worksheet says so.
3. **Declare the partition.** State what was counted as one event.
4. **File both signatures, never the average.** Averaging destroys the information that two
   competent readers, reasoning correctly, differ — which is *itself a measurement* of how far
   apart their priors sit.
5. **Report the zero.** Where a Custos assigns probability zero, that is the point at which
   convergence becomes impossible, and it must be printed. A disagreement that will never resolve
   is a different object from one awaiting evidence, and readers are entitled to know which they
   are looking at.

**Erratum 10, filed against the doctrine's own temptation.** It would be convenient to treat wide
intervals as ignorance awaiting more fieldwork. Sometimes they are. But where they arise from
divergent priors rather than thin evidence, *commissioning more feats will not narrow them* — and
X.2 §7's otherwise-correct instruction that "the interval is a purchase order" is silent on this
case. An interval owed to prior divergence should be marked as such, because ordering more
evidence against it is money spent on a result that cannot arrive.

## §7 — THE SAFEGUARD

Resonantism's structural risk is stated plainly in its own literature: a philosophy that can
reinterpret any opposition as resonance can be co-opted — *"your dissent proves me right."* For an
Order that holds the record of everything, this is not an academic worry. **It is the failure mode
that would make the Panscriptum a instrument of the very thing it was built to resist.**

The safeguard is the **Falsification Condition**, and it is binding on the Order:

> A critique that is (i) stated clearly enough to act on, (ii) raised across more than one cycle,
> and (iii) productive of **no structural change** — is a **refutation**, not a resonance, and is
> filed as one.

It works because it is externally checkable. Anyone may ask what changed. The answer is a matter of
record. A Resonantism that cannot point to changes is ossified by definition, whatever it says
about itself — and an ossified Resonantism is a contradiction in terms, which is the one
contradiction this philosophy does not get to inhabit.

The Order's own errata regime is the evidence that the condition is being met: Erratum 1 moved a
published Assay; Erratum 2 added three axes to a founding instrument; Errata 7 through 9 corrected
this session's own mathematics. *A cathedral that has never been repaired is not standing. It is
being remembered.*

## §8 — OPEN PROBLEMS

1. **Measuring prior divergence.** If two Custodes' priors are distributions, their divergence is
   computable — KL divergence, or total variation for the bounded case. A Ledger entry could carry
   *how far apart its signatories are*, turning "argued" from a vernacular adjective into a number.
2. **Detecting the zero.** Theorem 3 makes assigned-zero the decisive quantity, but nothing in the
   present worksheet format surfaces it. A Custos rarely knows which of their own priors are zero
   rather than merely small.
3. **Partition invariance.** Which findings survive a change of 𝒬? A quantity invariant across
   partitions is more trustworthy than one that is not, and this is checkable.
4. **Whether the Silence is a prior collapse.** The Pale erases registries. If a registry is a
   prior made institutional, erosion of the record is erosion of the capacity to condition on
   anything. Speculative; filed one desk up, beside the Architect questions.

## COLOPHON — WHAT HERE IS REAL

*Out of character: the Bayesian material is standard and load-bearing. Doob's martingale
convergence theorem and the Blackwell–Dubins merging theorem (1962) give the convergence result and
— critically — its precondition of mutual absolute continuity; the failure of convergence under
non-overlapping supports is elementary and permanent, since conditioning on a measure-zero event is
undefined. The reference-prior problem (Jeffreys; Bernardo; Berger) is the real form of the
Custodial Paradox: there is no uninformative prior, because "uninformative" is not invariant under
reparametrisation. The partition-dependence point is the standard objection to naive frequency
counting and is why 𝒬 must be declared. Resonantism itself is the owner's philosophy, corrected in
a companion document; its Falsification Condition is an addition made because the original named
the absorb-everything danger and answered it with a disposition rather than a test. The synthesis —
that a universal method with irreducibly plural priors is exactly the epistemic situation the
Panscriptum's grammar was built for, and that the Custodial Paradox is the reference-prior problem
wearing robes — is original to this document.*

*— Vol. 0.5, first fascicle. The school is one; the priors are many; the desk lamp burns on.*
