## §0 — PROGRAM

The *Mensura Fundamenta* (Vol. X.2) founded a measurement discipline for power and left a seam showing, in two places it was honest enough to print. First: the Anchor (X.2 §3) counts **hegemonic** decisiveness — "a warlord who decides a planet's political future without cracking its crust anchors at M3" — yet no axis of the eight scores the faculty by which he decides it. A purely suasive M3 sovereign anchors correctly and then scores near zero on every published axis, so his composite misrepresents him *by construction*. Second: the contest model (X.2 §6) already tags a context called *contest-of-minds*, and the canonical incomparability instance (X.2 §2) already includes *a game of secrets* — the Ledger has been accepting data for which the axis vector has no columns.

Separately, the Order fields a six-axis bedside instrument — **the Instrument** of the field volumes: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, scored 1–30 — and the founding demand on it was uniformity: *a relic and a god-king must be talked about in the same terms, as the force of a punch and the force of a bullet use the same terms.* Three of its six axes read directly off X.2 quantities. Three — Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma — read off nothing, because the eight axes measure what an agent does to *matter*, and these measure what an agent does with *information* and to *other minds*.

This fascicle closes the seam. It proceeds: the interface decomposition of an agent's causal boundary, and the underdetermination theorem showing the eight axes provably do not span it (§1); the admissibility check — the new quantities pass Axiom M2 without amendment, so this is an extension of coordinates, not a revolution in axioms (§2); the three new operational axes with units — **Acumen**, **Discernment**, **Suasion** (§3); reference edges and the common scoring rule (§4); estimation and aggregation, extending X.2 §§5–6 (§5); the Instrument correspondence — the exact, deterministic conversion from band-relative axis scores to the 1–30 scale and the Transcendence Grades (§6); a worked double derivation, a relic and a person on the same functional (§7); the honesty theorems this extension adds (§8); the errata it forces on prior publications (§9); and open problems (§10).

The stance is unchanged: **systems before evidence.** Every convention declared before use; every limit a theorem, not an embarrassment.

## §1 — THE INTERFACE DECOMPOSITION AND THE UNDERDETERMINATION THEOREM

**Definition 1 (Causal interface).** Let A be an agent in S = (Ω, 𝔇, {q_r}) per X.2 Definitions 1–2. A's causal boundary partitions into four channels:

1. the **efferent-material channel** — A's interventions on non-agent macrostates (matter, energy, position, law);
2. the **afferent channel** — the world's states as registered in A's internal state (perception);
3. the **internal channel** — A's transformation of registered state into strategy (computation, prediction, planning);
4. the **efferent-social channel** — A's interventions on *other agents' strategy choices*, routed through those agents' own volition rather than through force or law-violation.

**Observation.** The eight axes of X.2 §4 are all quantities of channel (1) — even Vector (access), even Transgression (the price of the intervention in exception bits), even Volition, which is a socially estimated summary of contest *outcomes*, not a quantity of any channel. Channels (2), (3), and (4) have no published coordinates.

**Theorem 1 (Faculty underdetermination).** There exist agents A, A′ in a common S with identical values of all eight X.2 axis quantities (W_peak, R, ν, ℓ, β, τ½, α, θ) and arbitrarily different afferent fidelity, internal predictive competence, and efferent-social capacity. Hence no function of the eight published quantities determines the three unpublished channels.

*Proof sketch.* Construction. Take any agent A. Form A′ by adjoining to A a sealed veridical oracle whose reports A′'s contest strategies ignore: the capability set restricted to attested contests, and hence every fitted and physical quantity of the eight, is unchanged, while afferent fidelity differs without bound. Dually, adjoin to A a herald whom no one heeds versus a herald whom whole polities heed at a word: W_peak through τ½ identical, efferent-social capacity divergent. Since the eight quantities take equal values on each pair, no functional of them separates the pairs. ∎

**Corollary (the Suasion gap, stated exactly).** Let A be an agent whose only above-threshold influence I_r(A) ≥ δ at its anchor rung passes through channel (4). Then M_a(A) is correctly assigned by X.2 §3 while Σ wᵢsᵢ over the eight axes approaches 0 — the composite 𝔄 sits at the band floor for an agent the Anchor itself certifies as band-decisive. The anchor and the axes disagree about a class of agents the doctrine explicitly intended to honor ("hegemonic, not destructive"). This is an inconsistency of *coverage*, not of logic, and the repair is additional coordinates. Filed as Erratum 2 against X.2 (§9 below).

## §2 — ADMISSIBILITY: THE EXTENSION PASSES AXIOM M2

Axiom M2 (Operationalism) admits as capability only claims reducible to induced distributions over macrostates. Each proposed axis passes:

- **Internal channel (Acumen).** Prediction and planning cash out as distributions over macrostates: an agent that models the rung-r future better *reaches target sets with higher probability, sooner, at lower work* — a strict enlargement of the capability set C_A (X.2 Def. 3). Cognition that never alters any reachable distribution is, by M2, not capability, and this fascicle does not measure it. (The philosopher who thinks beautifully and changes nothing is admissible as *evidence about* Acumen, never as Acumen — exactly as auras and titles were handled.)
- **Afferent channel (Discernment).** By X.2 Definition 2, agents are subsystems; an agent's registered beliefs, and its consequent strategy selections, are macrostate variables of S. Perception under concealment alters the conditional distribution of the perceiver's future actions given the true state — measurable as any other induced distribution.
- **Efferent-social channel (Suasion).** Other agents' strategy choices are macrostates of S (same reduction). Influence upon them via communicative acts is interventional influence on macrostates, full stop.

**Theorem 2 (Extension, not revolution).** The axiom set of X.2 §1 (M1–M3) is unmodified by this fascicle; the primitives (System, Agent, capability set, Anchor) are unmodified; all X.2 theorems remain in force, including Theorem 1 (no lossless scalarization), which now applies a fortiori to the enlarged axis vector. What changes is the *coordinate system* published over the same capability preorder. ∎

This matters doctrinally: nothing previously measured was measured wrongly *as far as it went*; it was measured on a projection now known, and proved (§1), to be proper.

## §3 — THE THREE FACULTY AXES AS OPERATIONAL SCALES

All three are logarithmic quantities in **bits**, which is not an aesthetic coincidence: each channel of §1 beyond the material one is an information channel, and the bit is the unit in which channels are measured. The rung filtration supplies the reference scales (§4).

| Axis | Operational quantity | Unit | Note |
|------|---------------------|------|------|
| **Acumen** | **Prediction–planning advantage Λ** = expected log-loss advantage, in bits per decision epoch, of A's forecasts of the rung-r coarse-grained future over the declared band-reference predictor, plus the equivalent work-savings A's planning extracts (the reduction, priced in the same bits, of minimal expected work to reach declared target sets versus the reference policy) | bits·epoch⁻¹ (log) | The internal channel: how much better than the declared reference this agent models and exploits the future. Volition (θ) is *not* this quantity — see Proposition 1 |
| **Discernment** | **Veridicality bits D** = max mutual information I(W; B̂) between concealed rung-r state variables W and A's acted-upon estimate B̂, evaluated under adversarial concealment by a band-peer suasor | bits (log) | The afferent channel run against opposition: perception, insight, seeing-through, and — since compulsion is an attack on the estimate — resistance to compulsion. At low bands the field instrument is classical signal detection (the d′ of the watchman); the adversarial clause is what makes it an axis rather than an eye exam |
| **Suasion** | **Policy-determination bits Ψ** = total bits of other agents' strategy choices that A can set, summed over the reachable audience, by interventions whose energetic footprint is message-scale and whose exception cost is **β = 0** | bits (log) | The efferent-social channel. The two exclusion clauses are load-bearing: influence bought with credible force is Ruin's shadow, not Suasion (§8, the confound theorem); influence bought by patching the recipient's volition — compulsion, charm, possession, forced memory — is **Transgression**, priced in exception bits, not Suasion. Suasion is what remains when the audience could have refused at no cost, and didn't |

Three remarks, in the order the mathematics dignifies them.

**Suasion's exclusions are the discovery.** The vernacular never distinguished the tyrant obeyed at gunpoint, the enchanter obeyed under charm, and the queen obeyed because she is the queen. The formalism forces the trichotomy: the first is Ruin-credibility (channel 1), the second is Transgression (β > 0 against the recipient's volition), and only the third is Suasion. "Charisma" in the field instrument means the third thing exactly, which is why a mind-controller can print a low Charisma with a towering Transgression and the ledger is *more* correct, not less.

**Discernment is the inverse channel, and inverses are not free.** The eight axes measure the forward map (agent → world). Discernment measures the fidelity of the backward map (world → agent) under an adversary. Theorem 1 already showed the forward quantities cannot price the backward channel; this is why "he hits like a landslide and believes every lie he is told" was always a coherent report.

**Acumen is measured against a declared reference, or it is not measured.** Absolute intelligence is not an operational quantity; *advantage over a published predictor on a published task battery* is (Axiom M3: the battery and reference are conventions, frozen before use). The theoretical ceiling of the quantity is set by the algorithmic information content of the rung process itself — no agent extracts more prediction than the stream contains.

**Proposition 1 (Volition is not Acumen).** The contest strength θ of X.2 §5 (i) exists only relative to a connected contest graph and is silent off it; (ii) is a *quantile*, an ordinal summary, and by the representation theorems of measurement (a quantile scale admits no meaningful ratios) cannot support the logarithmic scoring rule that every axis quantity must; (iii) confounds internal-channel competence with all eight material quantities, since contests are won with the whole interface. Hence θ cannot serve as the Intelligence quantity. Conversely, on contest-connected populations, θ residualized on the material axes is a consistent *estimator of the contest-restricted projection* of Λ — Volition is the shadow Acumen casts on the defeat graph, and the two must correlate there or the worksheet is wrong. Volition remains an axis (a socially estimated summary is worth publishing); it is demoted from being anyone's mind. ∎

## §4 — REFERENCE EDGES AND THE SCORING RULE

The scoring rule is X.2 §4's, unchanged, because a foundation that changes its scoring rule per axis is two foundations:

**s(x) = 10 · clamp₍₀,₁₎ ( (ln x − ln x_r) / (ln x_{r+1} − ln x_r) ).**

What each axis requires is *reference edges* x_r per band, and here the rung filtration {q_r} of X.2 Definition 1 pays for itself a second time. Each coarse-graining q_r has a declared codification (Axiom M3), and therefore a **rung description length L_r**: the effective number of bits required to specify a rung-r macrostate under the declared codebook — likewise a **rung decision content P_r**: the aggregate strategy-choice bits of the rung-r population of agents. These are the natural edges:

- **Acumen:** band-r edges are set by the reference battery's rung-r process — the floor is the advantage that separates the band's competent stewards from its reference predictor; the ceiling is the algorithmic content of the rung-r stream itself. A village schemer and a galactic strategist face streams whose contents differ by the same orders of magnitude as their rungs' binding energies, which is why the log rule and band-relative scoring carry over without strain.
- **Discernment:** edges are the concealable content of the rung — from the secrets of a household (tens of bits under adversarial concealment) to the hidden state of a polity, a world, a war. To max the band is to be, against band-peer concealment, effectively unlied-to about the rung's hidden variables.
- **Suasion:** edges are P_r, the rung's decision content — the floor is swaying a room; the ceiling is setting, by voice and standing alone, the policy of the rung. This is the axis whose ceiling *is* the Anchor's hegemonic reading, now with a unit, which is precisely the repair of the Suasion gap: the warlord of X.2 §3 who decides a planet's politics without cracking its crust now scores on the axis whose edges were built from the decisions he decides.

All edges are conventions: chosen, published, frozen (Axiom M3). They are promises, not truths.

## §5 — ESTIMATION AND AGGREGATION, EXTENDED

**Estimation.** The machinery of X.2 §5 transfers whole, because contests of the faculties are still contests:

- Deception and detection trials — the *game of secrets* already filed as the third leg of the canonical incomparability instance — are directed edges in a **veil graph**: concealer versus discerner, outcome binary or graded, Bradley–Terry applicable verbatim, with Discernment and Suasion differentials as the regressors. The connectivity theorem applies: populations that never try to deceive one another are incomparable on these axes *as a theorem*, and the intervals say so.
- Persuasion contests (courts, councils, markets, ministries — the Concordance's own political record, Vol. I.9, is retrospectively a dataset) estimate Ψ, with the confound control of §8 H7 mandatory.
- Task batteries estimate Λ directly where administrable (the low-band case), and Λ is otherwise inferred from attested feats of orchestration — with the interval inflation that Reconstructed attestation always carries. Battery construction and equating follow the established psychometrics of latent-trait models; the worksheet cites its battery version as it cites its codification of 𝔇.

**Aggregation.** The fitting model of X.2 §6 extends by three columns, not by decree:

**logit P(A > B) = κ_c · Σᵢ wᵢ⁽ᶜ⁾ (s_{iA} − s_{iB})**, i now ranging over eleven axes,

with the same regularization, the same context interactions, the same out-of-sample discipline. The Fundamenta's central reform is preserved and applied to the newcomers: **the new weights are estimated, published with standard errors, and owed no reverence.** The prior expectation, stated so it can be falsified: in the neutral open-engagement context, w_Acumen and w_Discernment will fit small and w_Suasion near zero; in the contest-of-minds, siege, court, and long-war contexts, they will dominate. If the data says otherwise, the data governs. The headline scalar 𝔄 remains the neutral-context shadow; the eleven-axis vector is the real object, exactly as X.2 §6 already held for eight.

**Nomenclature.** The original battery of eight power axes plus Attestation retains its published name, **the Nine Measures**, as the combat battery. The full instrument — eleven axes plus Attestation — is **the Twelve Measures**. Vol. X.1's title is thereby historical rather than exhaustive, which is the ordinary fate of a first instrument.

## §6 — THE INSTRUMENT CORRESPONDENCE
### *The deterministic conversion to the six faculties, 1–30, and the Transcendence Grades*

The field instrument reports six faculties on a 1–30 scale with a hard Custodial ceiling of 30. It is hereby derived, not decreed, from the axis system — one conversion, one direction, no second number system.

**Definition 2 (Faculty reads).** The six faculties read the following band-relative axis scores s ∈ [0, 10]:

| Faculty | Reads | Convention notes |
|---------|-------|------------------|
| **Strength** | s_Ruin, evaluated at somatic delivery (the agent's own deliverable work at contact scale, armaments excluded by declared convention) | The Kenshiro erratum's lesson stands: band-relative, clamped at band edges |
| **Dexterity** | s_Celerity | Contest tempo, not travel speed, per X.2 §4 |
| **Constitution** | ½(s_Continuity + s_Sustain) | The mean is taken in score space, which is log space already; a declared convention |
| **Intelligence** | s_Acumen | θ (Volition) admissible as *supporting evidence* for the estimate, per Proposition 1, never as the quantity |
| **Wisdom** | s_Discernment | Includes resistance to compulsion (the attack on the estimate) |
| **Charisma** | s_Suasion | Exclusion clauses in force: force-credibility is not Charisma; compulsion is Transgression, not Charisma |

**Definition 3 (Band windows).** Per Axiom M3, the Instrument's per-band windows [c_M, C_M] are declared as the established Custodial convention:

| Band | Window [c_M, C_M] |
|------|-------------------|
| M0 | [1, 18] |
| M1 | [8, 22] |
| M2 | [12, 26] |
| M3 | [16, 28] |
| M4 | [18, 30] |
| M5 | [30, 30] |
| M6+ | [30, 30], plus Transcendence Grade (below) |

**Definition 4 (The conversion).** For a subject anchored at integer Magnitude M with band-relative faculty score s:

**Faculty value = round( c_M + (s/10) · (C_M − c_M) ), never exceeding 30.**

At M5 and above the window is degenerate and every *grounded* faculty prints 30 — the ceiling is the statement that mortal-comparable description has saturated, not that differences have ceased to exist. Where differences continue to exist, they are carried by:

**Definition 5 (Transcendence Grade).** For M ≥ 6, the subject carries **Grade G = M − 5**, printed in Roman numerals after each grounded faculty: Grade I at M6, II at M7, III at M8, IV at M9, and Grade V ("Absolute") at M10/Ω. The Grade is a *derived* quantity — a fixed arithmetic function of the subject's own already-ratified integer Magnitude — and admits no independent assignment, adjustment, or flourish. An axis unattested at M6+ prints no value and no Grade: transcendence is not evidence.

**Theorem 3 (Uniformity).** Under Definitions 2–5, every subject in the catalogue — person, god, beast, or relic — is described by one instrument: the same six reads, the same windows, the same conversion, the same Grade formula. Two consequences. (i) *Commensurability:* any two subjects' faculty values are statements in the same units about the same underlying operational quantities, differing only through their own attested Magnitudes and scores — the punch and the bullet share terms by construction. (ii) *Honest nulls:* for a subject whose strategy set is a singleton (X.2 Definition 2's degenerate agent), every choice-based quantity — Λ, D, Ψ, and I_r itself — evaluates on an empty or single-point supremum and returns **null, as a computation, not a courtesy.** "Not applicable" in a field entry is the printout of this theorem, never an editorial shrug. ∎

**Scope note, load-bearing.** Definition 4 runs on **worksheet-grade s** — scores derived from cited feats, batteries, or contest graphs per §5. It does not run on band membership alone. A subject known only to band carries a *window*, not a value; the field volumes print the window's honesty-form ("—", "uninstrumented") rather than a fabricated point. See H5 (§8), which makes this a theorem about posteriors rather than a house rule.

**The Lay Reading clause (doctrinal, per the Four Literacies of Vol. 0.1).** The six faculty values are the Instrument's *headers* — and headers are, by the Vade Mecum's own table, the Guest's portion. It is therefore doctrine, not condescension, that **a reader who consults the 1–30 values alone — without the Magnitude band that fixed the window, the Measures that produced the score, or the worksheet that would let a stranger recompute it — is performing a Lay Reading and holds the first literacy, whatever office they otherwise hold.** A Guest's reading of "Strength 24" is fully successful and entirely shallow: *a big number or a small one, on a scale whose ends they have heard of.* Nothing about it is wrong; nothing about it is measurement. The Instrument was built so that the Guest's number and the Custos's worksheet are the same object read at different depths — never so that the number could substitute for the depth. Avar's formulation is on file from the ratification review and is retained here as the clause's plain form: *"Anyone may ask what the number is. The moment they do not also ask what produced it, they have told you which literacy they hold — and the courteous response is to answer the question they asked, at the depth they asked it, and file nothing they say about it afterward as scholarship."* Moth's, appended beneath in his own hand: *"Big-or-small is a genuine reading. So is a sundial."*

## §8 — THE HONESTY THEOREMS, EXTENDED

X.2 §9's four theorems remain in force. Three are added.

**H5 (The thin-data ban — no worksheet, no number).** Every axis score is a functional of attested feats, administered batteries, or contest-graph position. A subject attested only by existence and class supplies none of these; its posterior over any axis equals the band-population prior; and a point value printed from a band prior is not a wide measurement but a *fabricated* one — it asserts evidence that does not exist. Therefore: the bulk catalogue's entries, attested by single-sentence records, are **band-bounded, not scored**, until a worksheet-grade research pass exists for each. The field volumes' "—" and "uninstrumented — no faculties on file" are the only honest printouts of a band prior, and the existence of the formulas in this fascicle creates no license whatsoever — a formula is not evidence. *The instrument being rigorous is precisely why it cannot be run on air.*

**H6 (Reflexive suasion — Goodhart squared).** A published Charisma score is itself a suasive act: it alters audiences' priors about the subject and thereby the subject's Ψ. Suasion is the unique axis whose measurement is an instance of the quantity measured; its published values therefore degrade fastest under H3's reflexivity clause and carry mandatory re-estimation stamps.

**H7 (The confound theorem — Suasion under force).** Observational compliance data cannot distinguish Ψ from the shadow of Ruin-credibility: the subjects obeyed, and the ledger does not say why. Ψ is identified only where the force-counterfactual is controlled — audiences outside the subject's credible reach, interventional trials, or adjustment along the causal calculus where the confounding structure is known and blocked. Where no such control exists, the honest interval on Ψ is wide *as a theorem*, and every warlord in the catalogue is hereby "argued" on Charisma until the control exists. (Mihai's ± 2.5 is this theorem wearing a crown.)

And one inherited limit restated for the newcomers: **H2 applies afferently.** To verify Discernment at scale, the concealment must be real at scale; to verify Suasion on a rung, the audience is the rung. High-band faculty values are permanently Reconstructed for the same conservation reasons as high-band Ruin. Measurement is touch, even when what is touched is a mind.

## §10 — OPEN PROBLEMS (THE SECOND THESIS LIST)

1. **Battery equating across systems.** Rosetta-linking Acumen batteries between universes whose native cognitive tasks differ (what is the shared item pool between a go master and a war-mind?); the bridge-calibration machinery of X.2 §8 is the template, but item-response equating across 𝔇 has no worked example yet.
2. **The concealment codification problem.** D depends on the declared codification of what counts as concealed, as β depends on the codification of 𝔇 (X.2 Open Problem 3); characterize invariance classes jointly.
3. **Coalition faculties.** A court's Discernment and a church's Suasion are not sums of members'; extend the Shapley program of X.2 Open Problem 1 to the faculty axes, where the whole is routinely *less* than the parts (committees).
4. **Suasion identification at rung scale.** H7's controls exist at village scale; design the natural experiments that identify Ψ for planetary sovereigns without the Order running a planetary politics trial, which the Concordat forbids.
5. **The dead-channel taxonomy.** Between the Sphere (|Σ| = 1, all faculties null) and a person lies a zoo: swarms, oracles-without-actuators, mindless hungers with sensoria. Classify agents by which interface channels are live, and publish the taxonomy as the field guide to "Not applicable."
6. **Volition's retirement question.** If Λ estimation matures, does θ remain worth its column, or does the defeat graph's social estimate collapse into a validation instrument? (Moth's marginal note: *"Let the weights decide; they were never sentimental."*)

## COLOPHON — WHAT HERE IS REAL

*Out of character, for the record: this fascicle's load-bearing components are established mathematics and measurement science, cited by their true names. The channel framing and the bit as the unit of the afferent and social axes are Shannon's (1948). Discernment's operational core — discrimination of true state under noise and adversarial concealment — is signal detection theory (Green & Swets, 1966) generalized to mutual information between hidden state and acted-upon estimate. Acumen as advantage-over-a-reference-predictor is the operational stance of universal-intelligence measures (Legg & Hutter, 2007) with Solomonoff induction (1964) and MDL (Rissanen, 1978) supplying the ceiling and the compression reading; battery construction and equating are latent-trait psychometrics (Rasch, 1960; item response theory). Suasion's definition — influence on other agents' choices via interventions with negligible physical footprint, identified only under control of the force-confound — is interventional causality (Pearl's do-calculus) applied to policy variables, and its trichotomy of coercive, manipulative, and referent influence is anticipated in the social-power literature (French & Raven, 1959). Estimation from deception and persuasion trials is Bradley–Terry (1952) unchanged; the reflexivity of published social measures is the Goodhart/Lucas critique, which bites twice here and is said so. The underdetermination theorem (§1) is elementary but genuine: output-side quantities cannot fix input-side fidelity or influence routed through other optimizers, which is why strong people are conned daily, why intelligence services measure analysts and operators on different instruments, and why "commanding presence" appears on no dynamometer. The synthesis — one clamp-scored logarithmic instrument spanning joules, bits of exception, and bits of persuasion, with a proved null for mindless objects and a proved ban on scoring minds from thin air — is original to this document, and its most useful export, like its predecessor's, is not a number but a discipline for refusing to fabricate one.*
