# Timing log

Timing fields added to the annals (`annals/age_I..V.json`) so that one person's acts fall within a human life and the annals' own relative wording ("the following year", "that winter", "the same day") holds. No title, body, place, kind, canon field, id or order was changed; no canon event was given a field. Field meanings are in `reckoning.py` (`within`, `same_day`, `eve`, `between`). Each row gives the event, the field, the date the reckoning now assigns, why, and the phrase it rests on.

Counts: Age I 14, Age II 78, Age III 13, Age IV 24, Age V 5 (total 134).

## Unresolved, or left by design

- **Age II, Goraidh Mòr to Iain Garbh.** II-0077's window (2400–2250 BDE) and II-0093's (2000–1850 BDE) leave at least 250 years
  for Goraidh Mòr → his daughter Mòrag nan Rathad → her son Dòmhnall Crùbach → his niece Sìleas Dhonn → her son Iain Garbh.
  Each ruler's own acts are held to 40–50 years. The successions still fall about 80 years apart (II-0081, 0084, 0090, 0092 capped at 80).
- **Age II, Ealasaid Chiar to Beathag Mhòr.** II-0110 (1750–1650 BDE) and II-0135 (1300–1150 BDE) are too far apart for
  Ealasaid → Fearchar Òg ("rìgh after Ealasaid Chiar") → his great-granddaughter Beathag Mhòr. Fearchar Òg's reign is held to
  70 years (he took the throne as a boy and died old), and the extra time is placed before the wood-watchers' story with a window
  on II-0114. As a result, Ealasaid (1700 BDE) and Fearchar's division of the woods (1395 BDE) are about 300 years apart.
- **Age III, Aonghas mac Uisdein.** The windows on III-0084 (335–360) and III-0096 (420–445), together with his son's
  "short reign" (III-0097 ≥ 455), make Aonghas reign 355–445 and his father Uisdean 285–355. Alasdair Saor's span from first act
  to death (63 years) and Donnchadh mac Thormoid's (65 years) are set by the lower bounds of the windows on their death entries.
- **How the reckoning behaves.** A capped stretch piles up on its cap year: in Age II, 11 entries fall in 2815 BDE, and in Age I the
  Eilidh Ghlas and Oighrig stretch falls in 5189–5175 BDE. The entries after a capped stretch then return to the even spread,
  which leaves quiet gaps (Age II, 1209–846 BDE after Seonag Chrom). Adding fields alone cannot fix this.
- **Treated as later mentions, not acts, and not constrained.** I-0254 (Gilleasbuig Beag's island left empty); I-0223 (the Black
  Wind "remembered" at Ceann chiar); I-0211 (Achadh shean resettled: I-0135 leaves the date of the return open). The Stone
  Kings' list and the Red Hill list are treated as mythic apart from adjacent pairs, so Seonag nic Uisdein still follows her
  father Uisdean Mòr about 430 years later. Other later mentions: "rìgh after Ealasaid", "great-granddaughter of", "a descendant of".
- **Months come from a hash.** The Muileann ghlas column (VI-0112, VI-0113) still camps in June 2021 for a December battle. Only
  the clearing of the fair-ground (VI-0114) is tied to the battle, as its eve. Tolley arrives (IV-0160) a year after Crane dies,
  where the text says "months".

  V 3). `build_book.py` was not re-run, and dates quoted in the books or appendices were not checked.
