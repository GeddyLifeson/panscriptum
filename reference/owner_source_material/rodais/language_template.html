The Gaelic of Rodos

Ròdais

The tongue of the Ròdaich, spoken on Rodos in every age of its annals: in the harbour towns and the hill shires, in the courts of the kings, and in the copying-rooms of the library at Muileann chaol.

This book sets the language down whole. The sounds describes how Ròdais is spoken and how its spelling is read. The grammar in brief gives the language in a few pages; Word forms and Sentences give it in full, every example glossed word by word. Names tells how the places and peoples of the island are named, and Texts has conversations and tales with English beside them. The Dictionary can be searched from either language.

Ròdais keeps its old words. For the things the island knew before the humans came, about 1780, it says what the bards said. Some of what the humans brought kept the humans' own names, as bus and rèidio did. For the rest Ròdais made words of its own from old roots: a skyscraper is suathaiche-nèimh, “heaven-grazer”, and a computer inntinn-iarainn, “iron-mind”. Those words are marked ✦ in the dictionary.
