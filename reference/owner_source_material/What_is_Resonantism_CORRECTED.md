# What is Resonantism?

*Corrected edition. Substantive changes from the first draft are listed in the Errata at the end;
each is a factual correction, a downgraded overclaim, or a repair to an argument that handed its
critics a free win. Nothing in the philosophy's substance has been softened — where a correction
was possible, it made the claim **stronger**, because the accurate version of the evidence
supports the thesis better than the inaccurate one did.*

---

Resonantism is a philosophy that begins with a simple claim: **Being is constituted by relation.**
Every self is a web of resonances — to people, ideas, landscapes, histories, and even voids. We
are not isolated monoliths but cathedrals, scaffolding, ruins, and rubble simultaneously. This
complexity and tension are not a flaw but a profound beauty.

Resonantism is not the product of a single discipline. It emerges from Existentialism,
Phenomenology, Process Philosophy, Eastern thought, indigenous traditions, and art theory, toward
a single goal: to articulate why **relational tension**, not harmony or synthesis, is the essence
of existence. Think of it as a fusion of Nietzsche, Levinas, and Daoism, with the riffs of TOOL
and the crescendos of post-rock behind it.

---

## Art & Sound: The Aesthetic Foundations

Resonantism's sensibility is best grasped experientially, in work that weaves tension, climax,
and release — and, crucially, in work that **refuses release**.

**Penderecki's *Threnody for the Victims of Hiroshima*** is the purer case, and it is the one
that matters most. It does *not* resolve. Fifty-two strings move through tone clusters, extended
techniques, and glissandi, and the piece ends on a massive sustained cluster — unresolved,
unrelieved, held. This is the thesis in sound: tension sustained without synthesis, and no
promise that it will be redeemed.

**Górecki's *Symphony No. 3* (*Symphony of Sorrowful Songs*)** is the counter-case. It *does*
travel from grief toward something like consolation — a slow canon rising out of the bass, three
laments, an ending that is not quite comfort but is not despair either. Set beside the Penderecki
it demonstrates the range: constructive resonance and destructive resonance are both real, and
neither is the default.

**Diamanda Galás's *Plague Mass*** drives voice against noise, pushing the listener through
confrontation, collapse, and catharsis.

**TOOL's "Parabol/Parabola" and "Lateralus"** cycle through quiet build and explosive
polyrhythm, mirroring the rise–rupture–rebirth arc.

**Gothic cathedrals and Gaudí's Sagrada Família** display perfection and decay at once; ruins
whose arches frame emptiness and new growth do the same in reverse.

The mood these share is the core of Resonantism: beauty and horror, harmony and dissonance, rise
and collapse, held together **without final resolution.**

## The Four Axioms

### Relational Meditation
Identity is co-created through relation. To "meditate" here is to engage deeply — with people,
systems, landscapes, cultures — and let those relations transform you. There is no "true self"
waiting untouched inside; the self emerges through constant dialogue.

### Coexistent Contradiction
Contradictions are not bugs to eliminate but tensions to inhabit. Mutually incompatible positions
can, and often must, coexist. An atheist finds God, abandons God, hardens against God, or adopts
an idiosyncratic theology: each reversal is part of the same self.

**This is not a rejection of logic, and the distinction is worth stating precisely.** Formal logic
governs the relation between *propositions*. Resonantism concerns the relation between *lived
commitments held over time by a self that changes*. A person who believed X in 2015 and ¬X in 2025
is not a contradiction in the logician's sense; they are a trajectory. What Resonantism claims is
that the trajectory, not either endpoint, is the unit of selfhood — and that demanding a self be
consistent across time is demanding it stop being alive.

### Engaging Resonance
Value arises from engagement. Surface pleasantries and performative smiles are a kind of death.
Indifference — staying detached to avoid pain — produces a self that is alive biologically and
absent ontologically. Lived resonance includes love, anger, awe, grief, fear, and joy; each
encounter shapes who we become.

### Cyclical Impermanence
Everything — selves, societies, cathedrals — is impermanent and cyclical. Collapse isn't failure;
it is the precondition of rebuilding.

## The Metaphors

**Cathedral** — a coherent self or worldview you have built and share. Beautiful, ornate, and
always rubble-in-waiting.

**Ruin** — that self after rupture. Ruins are not voids; they incubate.

**Scaffolding** — the temporary framework assembled while rebuilding. Unstable by design, usually
dismantled once the new structure stands; sometimes reinforced and absorbed into the cathedral.

**Abyss** — the deep unknown faced when collapse strips the scaffolding away. Nietzsche's warning
(*Beyond Good and Evil* §146) applies, and Resonantism adds that the gaze can be generative as
well as annihilating.

These are meant literally and figuratively at once, because relation operates in both registers.

---

## Influences & Lineage

**Phenomenology & Existentialism.** Husserl's *zu den Sachen selbst* and Heidegger's
being-toward-death inform the focus on direct experience and impermanence — but Resonantism
rejects the search for any pre-relational essence.

**Levinas & Derrida.** The face of the Other and *différance* appear in the insistence on
asymmetry and difference *within* relation.

**Eastern & Indigenous Thought.** *Ganying* (感應, correlative resonance in Han cosmology),
*Ubuntu* (*umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu* — a person is a person through other persons), Nishida's
*basho*, and Daoist cyclicity inform the relational ontology.

**Deleuze & Guattari — the nearest precedent, and it should be named.** The **rhizome** is
explicitly anti-arborescent, non-hierarchical, and structurally resistant to schism: any point
connects to any other, and severing it produces new growth rather than fragments. That is
Resonantism's non-sectarian claim, arrived at first and by another route. **Peirce's fallibilism**
and **Whitehead's process metaphysics** are adjacent. Naming them is not a concession — a
philosophy whose stated virtue is conscious self-derivation cannot simultaneously claim to have no
precedent.

**Art and music, structurally.** Not decoration. Resonantism was born from extended pieces in
which time, tension, and collapse *are* the architecture.

---

## Relation to Other Fields

**Analytic Philosophy.** An analytic critic says contradictions invalidate a system. The
resonantist reply is the distinction drawn under Coexistent Contradiction above: propositions and
lived commitments are different objects, and consistency-across-time is a demand on the second
that only makes sense for the first.

*On the limits of formalisation:* Gödel's incompleteness results show that any consistent formal
system strong enough for arithmetic contains true statements it cannot prove, and cannot establish
its own consistency from within. This is **not** evidence for Resonantism — it is a theorem about
formal systems, not about selves. What it does establish is narrower and still worth having: the
demand that every system justify itself entirely from within its own resources is a demand no
sufficiently rich formal system can meet either. Resonantism claims no more than that.

**Continental Philosophy.** Kinship with phenomenology and existentialism; departure from
Heidegger's metaphysics in favour of relational metaphors.

**Physics and Neuroscience — by analogy only, and the analogy is flagged.**
Rovelli's **Relational Quantum Mechanics** holds that quantum states are relative to the system
doing the observing rather than absolute. **Synchronised neural oscillation** is a real mechanism
by which a brain integrates distributed activity.

Neither is evidence for Resonantism, and the first draft's use of them as support was an
equivocation: RQM's "relational" concerns state assignment between physical systems; Resonantism's
concerns selves constituted through encounter. They share a word, not a structure. They are
retained here as **illustrative analogies** — resonance is a useful image in more than one domain,
which is interesting, and not an argument.

## Critique Handling, and the Falsification Condition

The obvious danger: a philosophy broad enough to absorb every critique says nothing. Resonantism
answers with **withstanding-absorption** — letting opposing arguments press into the structure,
changing it without dissolving it. Pure absorption is formless; pure resistance is brittle.

**But a disposition is not a criterion, and the first draft stopped at the disposition.** Naming
the failure mode does not prevent it. So the condition is stated explicitly:

> **The Falsification Condition.** A critique that (i) is stated clearly enough to be acted on,
> (ii) has been raised across more than one cycle of collapse and rebuilding, and (iii) has
> produced **no structural change** in the philosophy across those cycles — is a **refutation**,
> not a resonance. It must be recorded as such.
>
> The test is not whether a critique feels absorbable. It is whether anything about the structure
> is different afterwards. **If nothing ever changes, nothing is being absorbed; the critique is
> being ignored, and calling that resonance is a lie.**

This gives the philosophy something it previously lacked: a way to lose. That is a feature. A
system that cannot lose cannot be trusted when it claims to have won.

### Authoritarian Risk

Because opposition can always be reinterpreted as resonance, there is a real risk of
co-optation: *"your dissent proves me right."* The first draft offered cyclical impermanence as
the safeguard, which is insufficient — it says an ossified Resonantism must collapse, without
saying when, or who judges, or what ossification looks like from inside.

The Falsification Condition is the operative safeguard, and it works precisely because it is
**externally checkable**: anyone may ask what structural change resulted from a given critique,
and the answer is a matter of record rather than of interpretation. A Resonantism that cannot
point to changes is ossified by definition, whatever it says about itself.

### Non-sectarian — a strength and a liability, stated honestly

Resonantism does not fracture the way Marxism-Leninism or Catholicism-Protestantism did: every
branch stays in resonance with the root, and disagreement deepens identity rather than destroying
it.

**This must be named as the double-edge it is.** A system that cannot be refuted by schism is a
system in which disagreement carries no information. That is the same property as
unfalsifiability, seen from a friendlier angle. The Falsification Condition is what keeps it a
structural feature rather than an immune system against learning.

## Application

**Personal life.** Crises are ruptures producing ruins and scaffolds. Embrace contradictory
impulses; refuse superficial placation.

**Politics & society.** Avoid facile centrism and dogmatism alike; both refuse tension rather than
inhabiting it. Reform and revolution are both cycles of cathedral and ruin.

**Art & music.** Buildup, climax, and dissolution as structure rather than ornament.

**Engagement, performance, and indifference.** Three postures, distinguished by what the person is
actually doing rather than by any diagnosis:
- **Engaged resonance** — the person is genuinely altered by the encounter, and alters it.
- **Performed resonance** — the forms of engagement without the alteration. Nothing changes on
  either side; the shape of relation is produced as an artefact.
- **Indifference** — no engagement is attempted. *"Like talking to a brick wall."*

*The first draft illustrated this with clinical categories — narcissism, psychopathy — and both
were used inaccurately. The distinction is about the posture, not about a diagnosis, and it is
sharper without them.*

---

## Why It's Not Just Rephrasing Old Ideas

**Method over doctrine.** Most philosophies revolve around fixed propositions. Resonantism is
primarily a method: embrace relation, dwell in contradiction, rebuild through collapse.

**Collapse as engine.** Dialectical materialism and Daoism recognise change; neither centres
*ruin* as generative. Resonantism does.

**Aesthetics as schema, not illustration.** It takes the temporal structure of art — rise,
tension, climax, decay — and makes that the schema of being.

**Conscious self-derivation.** Where syncretic systems paper over their sources, Resonantism names
them and says: the patchwork *is* the point. Derivation is unavoidable and valuable, and that all
identities are co-created is demonstrated by the philosophy's own composition.

*The first draft also claimed the non-sectarian ontology "has no real precedent." It does — see
the rhizome, above — and the claim sat badly beside the virtue of conscious self-derivation. The
honest version is stronger: Resonantism's contribution is not that no one thought of structural
openness before, but that no one made **collapse** the engine of it.*

---

## Conclusion

Resonantism turns contradiction, relation, and impermanence into virtues. It claims that
everything — art, politics, personal life — is built from rising and falling resonance. At worst
it becomes a ruin; at best a cathedral that invites others in.

Where it goes next depends on how it is lived: as a tool of exploration, or as a shield against
dissent. The Falsification Condition is the difference between the two, and it is the only part of
this document that its adherents are obliged to use against it.

## ERRATA — changes from the first draft

1. **Gomidas → Górecki.** The *Symphony of Sorrowful Songs* is Górecki's Third. Gomidas (Komitas
   Vardapet) was an Armenian priest and ethnomusicologist — a different person entirely. The first
   draft credited Górecki correctly on page 1 and Gomidas on page 5 for the same work.
2. **Penderecki's *Threnody* does not resolve.** It ends on a sustained cluster. The first draft
   described it as morphing "into aching resolutions," which is Górecki's arc, not Penderecki's.
   **Corrected in the thesis's favour:** an unresolved work is the better example of tension
   held without synthesis.
3. **The Gödel sentence was backwards.** The first draft had logic collapsing "where knowledge is
   complete and no unknown remains." Incompleteness runs the other way. Restated accurately, and
   the claim drawn from it narrowed to what it can actually bear.
4. **Neuroscience demoted from evidence to analogy.** Neural synchrony describes how a brain
   integrates information; it does not show that being is constituted by relation.
5. **RQM demoted from evidence to analogy, with the equivocation named.** "Relational" in Rovelli
   means state-relative-to-observer, not self-constituted-through-encounter.
6. **Deleuze & Guattari's rhizome named as precedent**, with Peirce and Whitehead; the "no real
   precedent" claim withdrawn as inconsistent with the conscious-self-derivation principle.
7. **The Falsification Condition added.** The first draft named the absorb-everything danger and
   answered it with a disposition ("withstanding-absorption") rather than a test. A philosophy
   with no way to lose cannot be trusted when it claims to have won.
8. **Clinical categories removed.** The narcissist/psychopath passage misused both terms and the
   authentic/inauthentic assignment was arbitrary. Replaced with engaged / performed / indifferent,
   which describes postures rather than diagnoses and does the same work more sharply.
9. **Non-sectarianism's liability stated.** A system unrefutable by schism is one where
   disagreement carries no information. Named rather than presented solely as a strength.
