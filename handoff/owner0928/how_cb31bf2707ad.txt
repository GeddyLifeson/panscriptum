SCALE, measured 2026-09-28: the fault was not 202 Digimon rows. 3,804 descriptions across 13 mode='web' records opened with wiki maintenance-template text. The biggest groups: Final Fantasy "This request can be discussed..." 1,596; Digimon 488; Zelda 366; SpongeBob 359; Transformers 308; Gundam 304; Regular Show 213; Halo 129; plus Left 4 Dead, Terminator, Fate, major-fantasy-pantheons and Roger Rabbit.

(a) MINER, src/wiki_source.py. There are two independent layers.
- Structural: _furniture_spans finds messagebox/ambox/mbox/notice/dablink/hatnote/cleanup/stub/maintenance/metadata containers, closing each by balanced tag counting and failing closed if one is unclosed. _paragraphs drops any <p> inside one.
- Textual: strip_banner_prefix cuts leading fixed-wording template sentences, and is_banner_paragraph drops a free-text "What's needed:" paragraph.

REPAIR, handoff/owner0928/repair_banners.py --go. It writes only through pipeline.write_record and is idempotent.
- Every old description was archived first, to handoff/_archive/owner0928_banner_descriptions.json.
- Fixed-wording prefixes were cut offline. 1,163 free-text or banner-only descriptions were re-fetched with the fixed miner. A re-fetch with no prose leaves description "" with thin_description True, and the entry is kept.
- Result: 3,804 repaired, and a re-run finds 0.

WRITER FIX, src/pipeline.py: _DESC_SNAPSHOT, a per-entry description watermark on the _TOP_SNAPSHOT pattern. write_record used to fold a stale holder's load-time description back over a repaired one on every entrypass batch. Now disk wins unless the caller actually changed the description.

(b) P8, pipeline._META_TERMS, tightened. It now refuses "what's needed", "the entry/page/record notes", "split into dedicated/separate pages", "... pages for each", "discussion page", "talk page", "by expanding it" and "remove this template/notice". The bare word "split" stays legal.

WRITER LAYER, src/generate.py. generate.py writes from the manifest, which is older than the repair. A pending job with any furniture entry is now held back and listed. If the detector cannot be imported, generate.py refuses everything.
