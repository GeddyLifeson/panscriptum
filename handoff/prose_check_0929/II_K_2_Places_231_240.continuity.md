# Continuity report: II.K.2/Places#231-240 (Digimon)

Coverage: canon has 10 entities; chapter has 10 entries (Crimson, Crusadermon (Cyber Sleuth), D-Brigade (Seekers), D-SAT, Daigo (Ghost Game), Daipenmon (Fusion), Damien, Dark Gennai, Dark King, Dark Master). None missing, none extra. Canon supplies only a one-paragraph description per entity, mostly a list-page blurb; scale_note empty everywhere; magnitude "unassayed" everywhere (all entries correctly print "unassayed"). Canon category is "Places" but every entity is type Character/topic Persons; the chapter follows the type (Class: Person), which is defensible.

## Findings

### HIGH

1. **Daipenmon (Fusion), shelfmark.** Printed `Ω › ? › ? › ? › ? › ? › ? › Dig,` -- truncated, missing the "Digimon [UNCHARTED -- Ladder-of-Being pass not yet done]" tail. Canon field `shelfmark` gives the full string. Placeholder violation.
2. **Daipenmon (Fusion), membership.** "part of the Bagra Army, which is led by the Three Generals". Canon description is about the Bagra Army's structure and never names Daipenmon or any member. Unsupported relationship (probably true to the source, but not in canon).
3. **D-Brigade (Seekers), series.** "a military unit within the Digimon World series". Canon description names no series (only Brigadramon, Cargodramon, Commandramon etc.). Invented source attribution.
4. **Daigo (Ghost Game), themes.** "explores themes of memory, identity, and the Digital World's hidden depths". Canon only says it is a list of characters from Ghost Game. Invented content claim. Echoed by MOTH: "narrative about memory and identity" (MEDIUM).
5. **Dark Gennai, conflation.** "his appearance in both his older and younger forms, highlighting the duality". Canon describes GENNAI's old and younger forms; Dark Gennai only "has a similar appearance" in black robes, purple turtleneck, pale blue eyes. Dark Gennai is not given two forms; description attributed to the wrong entity.
6. **Dark Gennai, invented role.** "often associated with guidance and wisdom, though his true intentions remain unclear"; QUILL: "offering guidance without revealing his true self"; "shifting forms". Canon is appearance only. Invented.
7. **Dark King, invented narrative.** "a ruler of the dark realm... influence extends over the digital world's most feared and chaotic forces"; "primarily that of an antagonist"; "opposition to the protagonists' efforts to restore balance". Canon: only "list of characters from the Digimon manga one-shot Digimon Get Back". Entire Record is invented. AVAR ("defined by his opposition") repeats it.
8. **Dark King, MOTH.** "His power is localized, not universal." Power claim with no canon; magnitude is "unassayed".
9. **Dark Master, invented scope.** "a title that encompasses multiple games and media... appearing in different forms across the Digimon Story and Digimon Data Squad series". Canon only mentions those series as separate list pages; nothing says Dark Master appears in them.
10. **Dark Master, invented role.** "mentor or guide to the protagonists... test the resolve of those who seek to master the digital world". Not in canon. Also "Unlike the Dark King, who is a singular figure" builds a comparison on the invented Dark King facts.
11. **Dark Master, QUILL frame break.** "I played through Digimon Data Squad -- he's a tough boss". Out-of-universe game reference and game vocabulary (Ground Rule 4), plus invented role.

### MEDIUM

12. **Chapter intro.** "towering cities of the Human World", "war-torn planes of the Digital World", "active participants in the unfolding of Digimon narratives". Not in any canon entry; the entries are all characters, none are places. Also "serves as a map" (banned shape).
13. **Crusadermon, membership.** "part of the Royal Knights sent by King Drasil". Canon names the Royal Knights collectively (Alphamon, Omnimon named); never lists Crusadermon.
14. **Crusadermon, allegiance.** "Crusadermon and other Royal Knights are divided in their allegiance"; AVAR "loyalty is as ambiguous". Canon divides the Knights in halves but does not place Crusadermon in either. "fractured timeline" overstates "Suedou reset the timeline".
15. **Crusadermon, UNNAMED HAND.** "Crusadermon's fate is a reminder..." No fate is in canon.
16. **Dark Gennai, UNNAMED HAND.** "those who appear in many forms" repeats the form conflation (see 5).
17. **Dark King, QUILL.** "I read the one-shot... a key part of the story's tension". Adds narrative significance; "pivotal element" in the Record likewise (also a banned word).
18. **Dark Master, Instrument line.** "Not applicable -- the Instrument measures beings, not Persons." Self-contradictory (Class is Person; every other Person entry says "uninstrumented -- no faculties on file", which is what the contract prescribes for unassayed beings). Template deviation.
19. **D-Brigade, "clear hierarchy, specialized functions, disciplined".** Canon supports Brigadramon commanding deployed units, but "well-organized and disciplined" and AVAR's "adaptable" are embellishment.
20. **Damien, Dark King, Dark Master: "his/he".** Canon gives no gender for any of the three.

### LOW

21. **Frame wobble.** Crimson, D-SAT, Daigo, Damien: "The entry points to a broader list of characters", "under cataloged", "Further details are pending classification". Talks about the entry/list rather than the world (Custodial hedge would be "the record names no role").
22. **Banned vocabulary (Rule 7).** "enigmatic" (Dark Gennai Record and AVAR), "pivotal" (Dark King), "testament" (D-Brigade UNNAMED HAND), "serves as a" (intro), "more than mere".
23. **Rule 5 (specific marginalia).** Crimson and Damien share near-identical AVAR and MOTH notes ("placeholder for a character whose role remains to be fully defined"; "felt more than it is understood"); several UNNAMED HAND notes ("more than they seem") are generic and reusable. Four Hands appear on every entry, though UNNAMED HAND was meant to be rare.
24. **Formatting.** Dark King and Dark Master lack bold names, `---` separators, and the two-space breaks used elsewhere; Dark Master's Instrument and Threads lines are formatted differently.
25. **Unsupported thin-data padding.** Crimson QUILL "missed opportunity for depth"; D-SAT "implies a role in the interconnected stories"; "deep and dangerous ambition" (Daipenmon). Vague flourishes.
