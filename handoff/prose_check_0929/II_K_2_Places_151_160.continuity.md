# Continuity report: II.K.2/Places#151-160 (Digimon)

Coverage: 10 canon entities, 10 entries in chapter, all names match, none missing, none extra.
Shelfmarks, Attestation ("Transcribed") and Magnitude ("unassayed") are correct everywhere. No assay decimals. All Threads read "pending the entanglement pass". Instrument lines match the unassayed rule for Persons and "not applicable" for the rest.

Severity totals: HIGH 13, MEDIUM 19, LOW 8.

Canon caveat: every canon entity carries category "Places & Locations", yet none is a place (characters, creatures, episode titles). The chapter cannot be faulted for that, but the intro compounds it (finding H1).

## HIGH

H1. INTRO (chapter frame). "towering ruins of the Digital World ... hidden sanctuaries of the Data Sea". Canon names no such places, and none of the 10 entities is one. Invented locations. Also "mythos", "franchise" (frame, see L1).

H2. CHIEF CABINET SECRETARY, Record. "ultimately clashing with Mitsuo Yamaki over the fate of the Hypnos system". Contradicts canon description: the clash was with the Head of National Security, who wanted to reactivate Hypnos. The Secretary thought that foolish without Yamaki, and had fired Yamaki earlier. Reversed relationship.

H3. CHIEF CABINET SECRETARY, QUILL. "I was there when the Hypnos towers fell. The Secretary was furious." Invented eyewitness event. Canon has the Secretary warning Yamaki beforehand, not a scene of fury at the towers.

H4. THE ASPIRATION OF THE APPMON!, Record. "a gathering of the Legendary Seven Code, a group of powerful Digimon". Canon description is only "The same guy from a week ago is after something from team Haru." "Legendary Seven Code" appears only in the title. Nothing says it is a group or Digimon or powerful. The Record also drops canon's actual content (a returning antagonist pursuing something belonging to team Haru) and recasts it as "the Appmon seeks to achieve something significant".

H5-H13. Invented first-person witness or combat claims in Quill's notes. No canon entry supports any of them:
- DOBERMON X: "I saw Dobermon X in action ... They cut through everything." Invented ability and event.
- LAVORVOMON: "I've seen Lavorvomon in action—it's a force to be reckoned with." Canon has only a romanization note.
- ERI IS MULTIPLYING BY COPY-PASTE!?: "I read this one—it's wild. Eri's story is a rollercoaster." Canon description is empty (thin_description).
- THE ASPIRATION OF THE APPMON!: "Appmon is on a mission." Not in canon.
- COUGARMON: "I've seen Cougarmon in action—it's a beast." Invented.
- TARGETMON: "I've seen Targetmon in action—it's a powerhouse." Invented.
- OGREMON X: "I've seen Ogremon X—it's a showstopper." Invented.
- RIKKA: "I met Rikka once—she was all fluff and fangirl energy ... made Himari smile." Invented meeting, personality, and event. Canon gives only "pampered friend ... latched onto Himari Oofuchi".
- LEO: "I fought Leo in a simulation once—his swordplay was precise". Invented duel and skill, and "simulation" is a game-ish frame.

(H3 above also belongs to this Quill pattern. Quill's notes are also nearly all the same "I've seen X in action" shape, which breaks Ground Rule 6.)

## MEDIUM

M1. DOBERMON X, Record. "significant evolution in the design and combat capabilities". Canon describes appearance only; combat capability is not supported. AVAR: "shift toward more aggressive and specialized combat roles" is likewise unsupported. The Record's "enhanced variant" is a fair paraphrase of "enhanced version".
M2. CHIEF CABINET SECRETARY, Record. "high-ranking official in the Japanese government". Canon says "Chief Cabinet Secretary" and never says Japanese.
M3. CHIEF CABINET SECRETARY, Record. "tasked with overseeing the containment of Digimon in the Real World". Canon: the Secretary told Yamaki that Yamaki's job was to eliminate Digimon. Overseeing containment is the Secretary's stated role in no canon line.
M4. CHIEF CABINET SECRETARY, Record. "played a critical role in the conflict against Vikaralamon". Canon: Yamaki alerted him, the Juggernaut failed, and Yamaki was fired. No critical role stated.
M5. CHIEF CABINET SECRETARY, MOTH. "one who underestimated the scale of the crisis". Not supported. Canon has Yamaki alerting him before the emergence.
M6. CHIEF CABINET SECRETARY, UNNAMED HAND. "His fate is a warning". Canon gives no fate. The description is truncated ("The situation was deemed a matte").
M7. LAVORVOMON, Record. "Its name and form are integral to the franchise's lore" and "suggests a connection to ... evolution and identity". Canon has only a romanization note and no form description.
M8. ERI IS MULTIPLYING..., Record. "explores the theme of duplication and identity ... both literal and metaphorical". Extrapolated from the title only. Canon description is empty. MOTH: "plays with the very nature of existence" is the same kind of extrapolation.
M9. THE ASPIRATION OF THE APPMON!, Record and MOTH. "a narrative of ambition and unity" / "story of ambition and the power of unity". Unsupported. Canon describes a pursuit.
M10. COUGARMON, Record. "reflecting its role as a predator in the Digital World". No role or habitat in canon. AVAR "predator in both form and function" and MOTH "strength and grace" are unsupported. UNNAMED HAND "Be wary of its claws": claws are not in canon.
M11. TARGETMON, Record. "making it a formidable opponent in battle", "blend of agility and power". Unsupported. "Formidable" is also on the banned list (see L5).
M12. OGREMON X, Record. "enhanced form of Ogremon" and "suggesting a unique role". The canon description does not say "enhanced form" (only the name and an X-FORM pointer) and gives no role.
M13. OGREMON X, Contradictions. Canon supplies competing versions: the original artwork had a normal axe, an updated one had an axe-guitar hybrid. The entry states only the hybrid and has no Contradictions section, so the template's requirement to log competing versions is missed.
M14. RIKKA, Record. "represents a minor figure in the narrative", "contributing to the emotional and relational fabric of the series". Neither is in canon.
M15. RIKKA, UNNAMED HAND. "even in fiction, it can shape the course of events". Explicit frame break (fiction named as fiction). Ground Rule 4.
M16. LEO, Record. "embodies the archetype of the warrior, a figure of strength and discipline ... symbol of power and resolve". Canon says only: tall, muscular, bipedal lion with a sword at his waist. Discipline and resolve are invented.
M17. LEO, MOTH. "The lion as a symbol of courage ... in many cultures". Real-world cultural reference, unsupported and out of frame.
M18. LEO, UNNAMED HAND. "shape the very fabric of a world". Banned phrase ("the very fabric") and a vague flourish.
M19. DOBERMON X, MOTH and AVAR. "its function within the Digital World remains unclear". Adds a Digital World frame not in canon. Minor, but reads as fact.

## LOW

L1. Frame wobble: "the Digimon franchise" (intro; Rikka; Lavorvomon "the franchise's lore"), "a character within the Digimon franchise/universe" (Rikka, Leo), "in the media" (Rikka, Leo). The style contract says franchise or title should become shelf or Collection.
L2. ERI, Record. "likely a manga or anime episode". Real-world medium guess, speculative and out of frame.
L3. Dobermon X, Chief Cabinet Secretary, Lavorvomon, Eri, Aspiration, Cougarmon, Targetmon and Ogremon X omit the "Contradictions:" line and the "Marginalia:" label (only Rikka and Leo carry both). Template deviation.
L4. Class inconsistency. Cougarmon, Targetmon, Ogremon X, Rikka and Leo are Class: Media and the Instrument reads "not applicable ... beings, not things". Yet Cougarmon, Targetmon, Ogremon X, Rikka and Leo are described as beings, while Dobermon X, Chief Cabinet Secretary and Lavorvomon (canon topic Persons) get Class: Person. This follows canon's topic field, but is internally odd. "Media" is also not in the template's Class list.
L5. Banned/stock words: "formidable" (Targetmon), "the very fabric" (Leo), "fabric of the series" (Rikka).
L6. LAVORVOMON, Record. "is a Digimon". Canon does not state what it is, only that it has an official romanization. Reasonable inference from the name.
L7. AVAR notes for Lavorvomon, Cougarmon and Ogremon X are generic ("a name with significance", "a predator in both form and function", "a fusion of form and function"). They partly fail Ground Rule 5.
L8. Intro claims "many remain uncharted" and "a selection of these places". The chapter contains no places, so the sentence describes something absent.

## Verdict
Fails the failure boundary (Hard Rule 1). Quill's fabricated first-hand encounters recur in nine of ten entries. The Chief Cabinet Secretary entry reverses canon (Yamaki was not the antagonist in the Hypnos dispute; the Head of National Security was). The intro invents places not present. The Aspiration entry misstates its canon. The rest is embellishment beyond thin canon.

Unverifiable: whether Appmon, Legendary Seven Code, Tooru (ceiling_entity, never mentioned in the chapter, fine) or Eri content is correct. Canon gives nothing to check against.
