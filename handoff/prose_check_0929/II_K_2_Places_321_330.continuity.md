# Continuity check: II.K.2/Places#321-330 (Digimon)

Coverage: 10 canon entities, 10 entries, names match in order. No missing or extra entries.
Shelfmarks, Magnitude ("unassayed"), Attestation, Contradictions, Instrument lines and Threads all follow the contract. No assay decimals, no invented shelfmarks, no asserted cross-franchise Threads.

Canon caveat: every entity has type "Character" under a Places category, and 5 of the 10 carry a description that is only a "list of characters from X" stub. Four carry the Appliyama 470 Election text, which is a scrape artifact, not a description of the named person or place. The prose invents a role for nearly every entity to fill that gap.

## HIGH

1. **Minako Sagamihara**: "a human girl who becomes a partner to the Digimon" / "Digimon Data Squad". Canon description is the Appliyama 470 Election (no series, no partner role, no gender). The Data Squad list belongs to Minnie Harris's canon. Entity conflated with another source entry. Traits "determination and loyalty" are also invented. Quill: "I met her once, and she was full of energy". Unnamed Hand: "Be wary of the app that gathers voter data" is canon-derived but attached to a person the prose has moved out of the election.
2. **Maho Yugawara**: "a location tied to the Appliyama 470 Election". Canon type is Character. No description says Maho Yugawara is a place. Also invented: "the Hands of Evil ... seek to manipulate the results for their own ends" (canon has only the heading "The Hands of Evil Aproach Eri!"), and "central point of interest in the Digimon narrative". Quill: "I was there for the Appliyama 470 Election ... a wild ride" is an invented event and attendance.
3. **Maiko Gion**: "a hub for the election's activities, where fans gather"; "the Hands of Evil, who seek to exploit the event". Neither is in canon. Quill: "I saw the election in action at Maiko Gion" is an invented event at an invented venue. Avar: "places like Maiko Gion serve as nodes in a network of influence and control" is an invented claim.
4. **Minnie Harris**: "often providing strategic insight and emotional support"; "defined by her intelligence and compassion"; "member of the team". Canon says only "list of characters from Digimon Data Squad". Quill: "always ready with a plan" is invented.
5. **Miho Sudou**: "a human who plays a significant role in the story, often interacting with Digimon and helping to resolve conflicts". Only "list of characters from Digimon Fusion" is supported. Quill: "a key figure in the team, always ready to lend a hand".
6. **Mika Kashiwagi**: same invented human/significant-role/conflict-resolver text as Miho Sudou. "Like Mika, she is a human" also asserts a relation between two entries. Quill: "a key figure in the team".
7. **Mika**: "As a human, she is part of the group of protagonists". Canon gives no species, gender or protagonist status. Quill: "brave and determined".
8. **Miki Nakajima**: same "human ... protagonists" claim as Mika. Quill: "brave and determined".
9. **Mimi's cousin**: "part of the broader cast ... friendship, loyalty"; Quill: "I met Mimi's cousin ... a key figure in the team". No such role is in canon. Avar: "the extended family of the Digimon world" is an invented claim.
10. **Miho (Re:Digitize)**: "Her presence"; "part of the broader cast that explores the themes of digital evolution, identity". Themes and gender are unsupported. Quill: "her journey is one of growth and discovery" is invented arc.

## MEDIUM

- Maiko Gion: "possibly a district or a venue where the election is conducted" is speculation from the name, which reads as an etymological fact.
- Minnie Harris, Unnamed Hand: "Minnie remains a clear example of the former [human]" is not a warning and asserts a fact. Avar: "contributions are vital but often overshadowed" is invented.
- Minako Sagamihara, Avar: "A standard human partner to a Digimon, her role is foundational" is invented.
- Marginalia across entries state themes ("emotional depth of the human experience", "fusion of human and digital existence") as if established in the entry.

## LOW / frame and voice

- Chapter preamble is off-template and breaks frame: "many of these entries are catalogued as Persons or Characters", "the franchise". It also asserts "significant locations", which is unsupported.
- Ground Rule 4 (in-universe only) breaks throughout: "anime series", "video game", "manga adaptations", "franchise", "series", "sequels". Miho (Re:Digitize) is described as "a character from the Digimon video game". Quill's "I watched Mika in action" treats the entries as a show.
- Banned machine tells: "not merely a celebration" (Maho), "underscores" (Miho Sudou, Minnie), "resonates" (Miho, Mika, Miki, Mimi's cousin, Minako), "interplay between" (Miho Sudou, Mika Kashiwagi), "highlights" repeatedly, "profound" (Maiko).
- Near-duplicate Records and Marginalia (Mika / Miki Nakajima / Mika Kashiwagi / Miho Sudou), against Ground Rules 5 and 6. Quill is built as "I saw/met X ... key figure" throughout.
- Stray "⌣" glyph before the Instrument line in Mimi's cousin and Minnie Harris. The final Threads line for Minnie Harris ends with a period.
- Class "Place" is not one of the template Classes, though it matches the chapter category. The Person entries sit under a Places heading (canon issue, not a prose fault).

## Unverifiable
Whether Maho Yugawara and Maiko Gion are places at all. Whether Minako Sagamihara is tied to the election. The species, gender and role of every Person entry.

## Verdict
Fails Hard Rule 1. Placeholders are clean, but the Records are generic invented role-filler and 10 of 10 entries carry unsupported concrete claims. Minako Sagamihara is conflated with another entry's source.
