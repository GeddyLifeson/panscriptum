# Continuity check: II.K.2/Places#1-10 (Digimon)

Coverage: 10 canon entities, 10 entries in chapter, 0 missing, 0 extra. Names match canon.
Canon is very thin: 9 of 10 descriptions are one sentence or one list-scope line; ExVeemon has appearance only. Almost every concrete claim beyond those is unsupported.

Placeholders are clean: all shelfmarks match canon exactly, all Magnitudes "unassayed", no assay decimals, Threads pending (no cross-franchise assertions), Attestation "Transcribed". Exception: template damage on the last two entries (see Espimon, ExVeemon, structural).

## HIGH

1. **Fusion, Adventure, Ghost Game, Cyber Sleuth** (Hard Rule 1). Each Record asserts "It includes both human and Digimon characters" (Fusion, Ghost Game, Cyber Sleuth) or "the original team of human children and their Digimon partners, as well as the supporting cast" (Adventure). Canon field `description` says only that it is a list of characters from the named series. Invented composition and roles.
2. **Ghost Game** (contradiction). Record: "the cast that populates the game's world"; AVAR "the game's characters"; QUILL "I've played through the game and watched the anime". Canon: Ghost Game is an anime series only; no game is given.
3. **Spiral** (invented). "existing as a form of artificial intelligence within the Digimon world", "a unique aspect of the Digital World's ecosystem". Canon: "Digital Lifeforms that mimic Digimon". No AI, no ecosystem, no location. Also QUILL "I've encountered Spiral in the games" (invented event/medium).
4. **ExVeemon** names (conflation). "officially romanized as "ExVeemon" ... American English media refers to it as "ExVeemon" as well". Canon names two sources (Reference Book romanization for Japanese media; Encyclopedia name for American media) but states no name for either; prose asserts they are identical. Unsupported, likely wrong.
5. **ExVeemon** marginalia. AVAR "a clear nod to its evolution from Veemon"; QUILL "I've seen ExVeemon in action—it's a powerhouse"; UNNAMED "unusual for a Digimon of its kind". Canon lacks evolution line, power level, or comparative claim on horns. Also "powerhouse" edges toward a scale claim on an unassayed entry.
6. **Espimon (Liberator)** (invented). "a unique entity within the Digimon lore", "companion and ally to the protagonist" (canon: Partner Digimon of Altea; Altea's protagonist status not given), "its role in the narrative is significant". QUILL "a key figure".

## MEDIUM

7. **ReArise, Survive, Beatbreak**. "detailing their roles, relationships, and contributions to the narrative"; "the people who shape its lands". Canon: bare list. Invented contents and a "lands" setting.
8. **Fusion**. AVAR "focus on its protagonists and their allies"; **Adventure** AVAR "emphasis on its core group of protagonists". Unsupported.
9. **Class mismatch, Spiral**: Class Event for "Digital Lifeforms" (canon type Character, topic Events). Lists as Event is defensible via topic; Spiral is not.
10. **Espimon / ExVeemon marginalia speculation**. MOTH "may represent loyalty or guidance"; "might symbolize duality or transformation"; "highlighting the bond between Digimon and their human partners". Embellishment reading as fact.
11. **Spiral** MOTH/UNNAMED: "role remains underdeveloped"; "the systems that shape them". Unsupported claims about role and systems.
12. **Cyber Sleuth, Fusion, Survive, Beatbreak** QUILL claims of having read/played/watched the specific media, "related materials" (Survive/Beatbreak), and "the manga" (Fusion, present in canon). Invented first-person acts, and out-of-universe media framing.
13. **Ghost Game / Cyber Sleuth** Record: "reflecting the series' narrative focus" and Cyber Sleuth called "series" though canon says game. Minor category slippage.
14. **Structural template violations (Espimon, ExVeemon)**: literal unfilled "Not applicable -- the Instrument measures beings, not [places/things/events]" printed, followed by a stray Instrument line (ExVeemon) or "▣ Threads" header (Espimon); Threads printed twice on Espimon; no Record heading, "Contradictions:" mispunctuated.

Violates Ground Rule 5/6 (not fact findings).

## Vocabulary
"Digital Lifeforms" rendered as "artificial intelligence"; "Partner Digimon" preserved; "Digital World" introduced for Spiral, not in canon.

## Unverifiable
Whether ExVeemon's two official names differ (canon description is truncated of the names). Whether Altea is a protagonist.

## Verdict
Fails Hard Rule 1. Structure, shelfmarks, magnitudes and Threads are compliant; content is heavily padded with unsupported composition claims and first-person marginalia asserting invented experiences. Counts: HIGH 6 clusters, MEDIUM 8, LOW 6.
