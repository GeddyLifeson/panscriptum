# Continuity check: II.L.7.4/Events#121-130 (Arcanum Worlds, Odyssey of the Dragonlords)

Coverage: canon 10 entities, chapter 10 entries, in canon order. No missing entries, no extra entries.
Placeholders: all 10 shelfmarks match canon exactly; all Magnitudes "unassayed"; all Attestation "Transcribed"; all Threads "pending". No assay decimals. No placeholder violations.

## HIGH (invented or contradicted fact, frame break on a canon-supplied game term)

1. Great Relay: "participation granting 900 XP to the victors". Canon: "Participation awards 900 XP" (all participants, not victors). Contradiction; "XP" is also a game term (Ground Rule 4 frame break). Also invented: "perilous", "marking their transition from competitors to champions".
2. Corruption of Lutheria: "an event to be run during the adventure". Canon description is literally "event to be run during the adventure." Copied game-master frame language into in-world prose (Rule 4). Everything else is invented: "moral and political upheaval", "test of the participants' resolve", "struggle for power". Canon supports nothing beyond the name.
3. Eternal Apples: "described as both a natural wonder and a mystical artifact ... reverence and contention among those who seek its power". No canon support (description: trees on "the island", fresh until eaten, effect of a potion of healing). Also "on an unnamed island" (canon says only "the island"), and "potion of healing" is a game term left unsubstituted (Rule 4).
4. Eternal Apples, QUILL: "a traveler describe the apples as 'the breath of the island itself'". Invented quotation and witness.
5. Blessed Olive Oil: "Unlike the apples, which are naturally occurring", "a rare substance", "ritual of devotion", "island's deep spiritual significance", "mythic status". None in canon. "same island as the Eternal Apples" is a plausible reading of "the island" but is an inference.
6. Blessed Olive Oil, QUILL: "I once helped in the making of this oil ... physical and spiritual toll". Invented participation and outcome.
7. Battle of Mytros: "shaping the future of the world", "the cost of peace"; MARGIN QUILL "I've read accounts ... turning point in history"; AVAR "redefines the balance of power". Canon says only: occurs after the Oath of Peace ends, determines the outcome of the Titans' war on Thylea. Asserts consequences not in canon.
8. Arcana: "forming the foundation of magical and arcane knowledge", "the language of power". Canon: only "a system that can be studied and granted proficiency in". Invented role.
9. Hergeron: "a force of destruction", QUILL "stories of Hergeron's wrath", UNNAMED "spoken in both awe and dread". Canon: "Titan, Lord of Battle." Only Titan and war-lordship are supported; wrath, destruction, awe/dread are added.

## MEDIUM (unsupported embellishment that reads as fact)

- Dexterity bonus: "reflects the precision and speed of the wielder", "Though it is a minor power, its impact on battlefield dynamics is notable". Canon gives only the multiplier-by-3 on 120 ft javelin range and "the competitor's choice". Also "battlefield" conflicts with a Great Games competitor context.
- Great Relay: "a test of endurance, speed, and strategy". Not in canon.
- Award Ceremony: "a moment of celebration, honor, and recognition". Core facts (Acastus, silver dragon, medals, laurels) are faithful; UNNAMED "comes at a cost" is unsupported.
- Tavern Sponsorships: "heroes' success is monetized and their influence extended beyond the battlefield". Canon: sponsorship deals bringing business to taverns.
- Battle of Mytros: "a moment of great conflict and consequence".
- Marginalia claiming first-hand witness across entries (Quill: "seen it in action", "watched the Great Relay", "stood in the crowd", "seen how these sponsorships can change a hero's life"). New concrete claims about events. Quill also asserts effects of Arcana "firsthand".
- Moth on Arcana ("deep, underlying order"), Great Relay ("world's values"), Award Ceremony ("reverence for achievement"): generic values claims not in canon.
- Corruption of Lutheria, MOTH: "struggle between good and evil"; Battle of Mytros, MOTH: "order and chaos". Invented thematic content.
- Blessed Olive Oil, AVAR: "valuable and rare". Invented.
