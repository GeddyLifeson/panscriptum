# Continuity check: II.L.7/Factions (Xanathar's Guide to Everything)

Canon: 1 entity, "Xanathar's Thieves' Guild / Skullport" (Organization / Location; Transcribed; unassayed). The description says only: the criminal organization Xanathar leads, the underdark port beneath Waterdeep where his lair is, referenced as scene-setting for the narrator framing device.

Chapter: 3 entries (Oath of Detachment, Neutrality Versus Everything Else, Oath of the Directive). None is the canon entity.

Verdict: the chapter was written against a different source's data (a "A Plethora of Paladins" paladin-archetype book). Effectively total invention. Not a rare-invention case.

## Findings

### Missing / extra entries (HIGH)
- MISSING: "Xanathar's Thieves' Guild / Skullport" has no entry. Class should be Polity or World (Organization / Location).
- EXTRA: all three entries (Oath of Detachment, Neutrality Versus Everything Else, Oath of the Directive) are not in canon.

### Contradictions with canon (HIGH)
- Entire chapter subject. Frame text: "the spine code II.L.7 of A Plethora of Paladins" contradicts canon source_name "Xanathar's Guide to Everything".
- Shelfmarks on all three entries end "A Plethora of Paladins [UNCHARTED ...]" instead of "Xanathar's Guide to Everything [UNCHARTED ...]". Canon shelfmark was altered (source is wrong).
- Chapter blurb: "detail three such orders" of paladins. Canon has one criminal organization / port.
- Class "Praxis" for all three; canon type is Organization / Location.

### Invented facts (HIGH), all entities not in canon, nothing supported by any canon field
- Oath of Detachment: "rejects the polarized conflicts of cosmic forces"; "target dragons and giants"; "knight-arcanists, paramanders, or impartialists"; "dyed hair, ambidexterity".
- Neutrality Versus Everything Else: "detecting imbalance and smiting the radical"; "arcane foci"; "divine smite".
- Oath of the Directive: "mechaknights and android masters"; "the Automaton Principles"; "loyalty to their maker".
- Chapter blurb: "philosophies given form, each with its own principles, quirks".

### Marginalia (MEDIUM)
- Notes are commentary on invented Records, so they inherit the problem. No first-person events found.

### Placeholder violations (LOW)
- Shelfmarks use the `Ω › ? ›` form correctly (apart from the wrong source name). Magnitude "unassayed" and Threads "pending" are correct. No decimals.
- Instrument line is "measures beings, not practices" (non-template wording; template says "things"). Entry 3 uses the correct "things" and a doubled "Threads" header. Minor format drift.

### Frame breaks (MEDIUM)
- Records use game-mechanics terms: "divine smite", "arcane foci", "abilities", "class", "archetype", "quirks", and the blurb says "mechanical foundations". This breaks Ground Rule 4.
- "compendium of paladin archetypes" is meta-catalogue language.
- "not merely groups of warriors, but philosophies" is a banned sentence shape. The first Record says "known as", which imports outside knowledge.
